title: "Self-hosting on your NAS"
description: "Community guides for running Reactive Resume v6 on a Synology or UGREEN NAS with Docker."
---

Lixandru Marius Bogdan ([@mariushosting](https://github.com/mariushosting)) has written step-by-step guides for running
Reactive Resume v6 on a Synology or UGREEN NAS. Both guides deploy a Docker Compose stack through Dockhand with the
app, PostgreSQL 18 and SeaweedFS for file storage, set up HTTPS through a reverse proxy, send email through Gmail SMTP,
and finish by turning off new sign-ups. Thank you, Marius, for writing and maintaining them.
