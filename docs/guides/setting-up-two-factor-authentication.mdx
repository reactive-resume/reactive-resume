---
title: "Setting up two-step verification"
description: "Turn on two-step verification in Reactive Resume with an authenticator app, save your backup codes, and turn it off again when you need to."
---

Two-step verification (also called two-factor authentication, or 2FA) adds a second check when you sign in: after your password, you enter a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone or computer. Someone who learns your password still can't get in without it.

In the native app, select **Settings → Account → Two-step verification** to open its section in the authenticated in-app browser. [Use the same account as the app](/guides/updating-your-profile#manage-sign-in-security-in-the-native-app).

## Before you start

- Install an authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, Bitwarden or Authy.
- Your account needs a password. If you signed up with a social account, the **Two-step verification** row asks you to [set a password](/guides/updating-your-profile#change-your-password).

## Turn on two-step verification

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sign-in and security">
    Select your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open **Settings**. On the **Account** page, find **Two-step verification** under **Sign-in and security**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn the switch on and enter your password">
    The **Turn on two-step verification** dialog asks for your **Password**. Enter it and select **Continue**.

    <Frame caption="Start two-step verification">
      <img src="/images/guides/setting-up-two-factor-authentication/enter-password.webp" alt="Password confirmation before setting up two-step verification" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your backup codes">
    **Save your backup codes** shows 10 one-time codes, in capitals and digits that don't look alike. Select **Download codes** to save them as a text file, or **Copy codes** to paste them somewhere safe, such as your password manager. Then select **Continue**.

    <Frame caption="Save your backup codes; example secrets hidden">
      <img src="/images/guides/setting-up-two-factor-authentication/backup-codes.webp" alt="Save your backup codes dialog with ten example code values hidden, Download codes, Copy codes and Continue" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Reactive Resume to your authenticator app">
    In **Set up your authenticator app**, scan the QR code with your app. If you can't scan it, use the key under **Can't scan? Enter this key in the app**: it's shown in groups of four, and the copy button next to it copies the whole key.

    <Frame caption="Scan or copy your authenticator secret; example secrets hidden">
      <img src="/images/guides/setting-up-two-factor-authentication/scan-qr-code.webp" alt="Authenticator setup dialog with a hidden QR code above a hidden secret key, Copy secret, six code boxes, Cancel and Continue" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the 6-digit code">
    Type the code your app shows for Reactive Resume; the first box already has focus. The dialog verifies it as soon as the code is complete, or you can select **Continue**. A wrong code is cleared so you can type the next one. An accepted code turns on two-step verification and closes the dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The switch now shows two-step verification is on. From your next sign-in, Reactive Resume asks for a code after your password.

<Frame caption="Two-step verification turned on">
  <img
    src="/images/guides/setting-up-two-factor-authentication/two-step-verification-on.webp"
    alt="Account settings with two-step verification enabled and standalone Profile navigation"
  />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Save the backup codes before continuing to the authenticator step. They are shown only once. Two-step verification
  stays off until your authenticator code is accepted. If you later lose your authenticator app and have no backup
  codes, you can't sign in with your password.
</Warning>

## Sign in with a code

After your password, enter the code from your app on the **Two-step verification** page. If you don't have your app, select **Lost access to your authenticator?** and enter a backup code. Each backup code works once. See [Signing in](/guides/signing-in#enter-a-two-step-code) for details.

Signing in with a [passkey](/guides/setting-up-passkeys) or a linked social account doesn't ask for a code.

## Turn off two-step verification

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn the switch off">
    In **Sign-in and security**, turn off **Two-step verification**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm with your password">
    In **Turn off two-step verification?**, enter your **Password** and select **Turn off two-step verification**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Turning off two-step verification needs your password">
  <img
    src="/images/guides/setting-up-two-factor-authentication/disable-dialog.webp"
    alt="Turn off two-step verification dialog with an empty Password field and confirmation button"
  />
</Frame>

To move to a new phone, turn two-step verification off and on again, then scan the new QR code on the new device. Your old codes and backup codes stop working.
