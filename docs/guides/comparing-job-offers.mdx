---
title: "Choose between two offers"
description: "Compare saved offers against your preferences, keep unknown terms visible and prepare questions before deciding."
---

One offer pays more. Another is remote. Both mention options, but neither explains their value. **Career → Offers** puts the terms side by side so you can see what is known and what you still need to ask.

## Save each offer

Choose **Add offer** in **Career → Offers**, or in the relevant application's **Messages** tab. Select an application, enter the three-letter currency code and pay period, then add the terms you know. Base salary, bonus and learning budget can stay blank when unknown; enter `0` only when the amount is confirmed as zero. Optional fields include bonus conditions, equity, location, office days, leave, other benefits and a response deadline in your account timezone. Choose **Save offer**. Manual entry needs no AI connection and sends no terms to an AI provider.

Use full amounts such as `95000` rather than `95k`. Errors appear after leaving a field or choosing **Save offer**, and your entered terms stay available for correction. A response deadline must be a valid local time in your account timezone; times skipped by daylight saving cannot be saved. On mobile, wait for the account timezone to load, retry a failed load, or clear the optional deadline.

To extract terms with an AI connection instead:

1. Open the relevant application's **Messages** tab.
2. Paste the offer's terms and select **Read it for me**.
3. Review the proposed offer information against the original message or document.
4. Select the offer change you want to keep, then choose **Apply … change(s)**.

Repeat for the other application. Written and verbal offers can both be saved. If a term was read incorrectly, enter a corrected offer manually or clarify the pasted terms and generate a corrected proposal before saving it. You can delete an unwanted saved version from **Library**, with **Undo**.

Saving terms records an offer; it does not accept it or reply to the employer.

## Choose the offers to compare

Open **Career → Offers**. Select the company heading on either side to choose a saved offer and its version. **Swap** exchanges the sides. If you have only one offer, you can still review its missing details while the other side is empty.

## Read differences carefully

Compare base salary, bonus, equity, where you work, leave, learning budget, other benefits and response deadlines.

- Money stays in the offer's original currency and pay period. Differences are shown only when those terms match.
- **Unknown** means the offer does not state the term; it does not mean zero.
- A conditional bonus is still conditional. Equity wording is not a confirmed cash value.
- Totals are not added together. Read the original documents before treating several figures as one package.

The comparison itself uses saved data and makes no AI request.

## Check what matters to you

**Your requirements** uses preferences from **Career → Knowledge → Preferences**, such as minimum base salary, locations and maximum office days. Keep them current before comparing.

A higher salary may still come with more office days than you want. A remote offer may leave its working hours or travel expectations unstated. Write down what would change your choice.

## Ask about missing terms

Under **Not in either offer yet**, read the suggested questions and who should answer them. Use **Copy questions** to take them to your own email or notes.

Copied questions include their intended recipients. Offer selections stay with the coaching conversation in this browser when you return through Recent; review the context chip before sending another message.

Choose **Plan what to ask** to open the coach with both offers as context. Review its context and privacy line before sending. You can ask for a conversation plan or a negotiation draft; you decide what to send and which offer to choose.

If updated terms arrive, save another offer version and compare that version. The comparison does not replace the original offer documents or record an acceptance on your behalf.
