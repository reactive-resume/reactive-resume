---
title: "Choose between two offers"
description: "Compare saved offers against your preferences, keep unknown terms visible and prepare questions before deciding."
---

One offer pays more. Another is remote. Both mention options, but neither explains their value. **Career → Offers** puts the terms side by side so you can see what is known and what you still need to ask.

Offer-stage applications appear first in **Add offer**; applications closed as **Not selected** are omitted. Accepted applications remain available, with **Accepted** shown in the picker and comparison. Closing an application as accepted records the outcome, not the offer terms: add those separately. Accepted offers have no questions to ask before deciding.

**Compare offers** in an application's workspace opens that application's newest terms on side A. The default comparison uses the newest version of each job. Pick older versions deliberately to review history. **Latest** marks the newest saved version; the version number distinguishes saves made in the same minute. Two versions of one application show **Changes since…** and signed money changes against the other version, without **Ask both**.

## Save each offer

Moving an application to **Offer** shows **Add the offer's terms** in the confirmation, which opens this form in its **Messages** screen.
Choose **Add offer** in **Career → Offers**, or in the relevant application's **Messages** screen. An offer belongs to an application: with none yet, the button reads **Add an application first** and opens **Save a job**. Select an application, choose a currency by its code and localized symbol, and check the pay period. A job's salary line supplies starting currency, period and base when it states them. A salary amount without a named period starts at **Per year**; check it before saving. Otherwise currency starts from your latest saved offer, then your minimum salary in Preferences; the pay period follows Preferences until you choose one. Changing the application or loading Preferences later never replaces a period you explicitly selected. Enter the base salary and response deadline only when you know them. Base salary, bonus and learning budget can stay blank when unknown; enter `0` only when the amount is confirmed as zero. A deadline asks for a time too, in your account timezone. **More options** holds the offer type (written or verbal), bonus, bonus conditions, equity, learning budget, office days, location, leave and other benefits. Choose **Save offer**. For applications before **Offer**, saving moves them to **Offer** with **Undo**, which keeps the offer and restores the prior stage. The newest terms stay visible in **Messages**, with **Compare**. Manual entry needs no AI connection and sends no terms to an AI provider.

Use full amounts such as `95000` rather than `95k`. Bonus also accepts a percentage of base, such as `10%` or a decimal percentage in your interface locale. It stays a percentage when saved and reopened. With a known base, Offers shows its money equivalent in that base's currency and pay period; with no base, it shows only the percentage. Errors appear after leaving a field or choosing **Save offer**, and your entered terms stay available for correction. A response deadline must be a valid local time in your account timezone; times skipped by daylight saving cannot be saved. On mobile, wait for the account timezone to load, retry a failed load, or clear the optional deadline.

To extract terms with an AI connection instead:

1. Open the relevant application's **Messages** screen.
2. Paste the offer's terms and select **Read it for me**.
3. Review the proposed offer information against the original message or document. The proposal lists every term it extracted, including the base, any bonus and its conditions, equity, the start date and the employer's stated answer deadline; a term it could not find stays unknown rather than being guessed.
4. Select the offer change you want to keep, then choose **Apply … change(s)**.

Repeat for the other application. A response deadline the employer states belongs in **Respond by**; an unrelated follow-up is never copied into it. Written and verbal offers can both be saved. If a term was read incorrectly, open the offer's heading in **Career → Offers** and choose **Edit terms**; the form opens with the saved terms and saves your correction as a new version. The earlier version stays available. If that same saved version's terms changed in another tab or device since you opened it, saving asks you to review the latest terms first. Close the editor and open **Edit terms** again to start from that latest copy. **Delete offer** in the same menu removes a version, with **Undo**; you can also delete it from **Saved**.

Saving terms records an offer; it does not accept it or reply to the employer.

If a save reports a connection problem, retrying the identical edit for that version recovers its saved result. After a reload or app restart, reopen the same version and re-enter those terms if needed; the form itself is not restored. Leaving while a save finishes keeps the page you opened. The saved version remains available in Offers; native Offers selects and highlights it when you return, keeping your comparison.

An application you moved to the **Offer** stage without saving terms appears in **Career → Offers** under **In the Offer stage, terms not added**, on web and in the native app. Each has its own **Add offer**, with the application already chosen. Nothing is compared, and no terms are filled in, until you enter them.

Offers loads saved terms, preferences and application metadata separately. Saved offers and their comparison stay visible while application metadata loads or fails. The affected section shows an error with **Try again** on web or **Retry** in the native app. Retry reloads that account's data; an unavailable application list is never presented as an empty list or an AI setup step.

## Choose the offers to compare

Saving from Offers opens the new version on side A and briefly highlights its heading. An explicitly chosen side B stays selected.

Open **Career → Offers**. Select the company heading on either side to choose a saved offer and its version. Phone comparison rows repeat the company and role so two jobs at the same company stay distinguishable. **Swap** exchanges the sides. If you have only one offer, you can still review its missing details while the other side is empty.

Empty comparisons include a **Messages** chooser. Select the application to open its **Messages** screen without starting an AI request. For optional AI reading, paste the offer email, choose **Read it for me**, approve the request and review the proposed terms before applying them. You can always enter terms manually instead. Closing an application as accepted does not add its terms.

## Read differences carefully

Compare base salary, bonus, equity, where you work, leave, learning budget, other benefits and response deadlines.

- Money stays in the offer's original currency and pay period. Differences are shown only when those terms match. For the same currency, a monthly base beside a yearly offer shows the arithmetic for 12 months. That hint does not compare the offers; hourly pay needs working hours and has no guessed yearly equivalent.
- With one offer, the section is **Open questions**. With two, it is **Not in either offer yet**.
- A missing term says where the offer came from. **Not found in the message** means the terms were read from an employer's message and that term wasn't found; check the message, as the reading can miss it. **Left blank** means you entered or corrected the terms and left it empty. **Not recorded** marks offers saved before the source was kept. None of them means zero.
- Each offer's heading says whether it was **Read from a message**, **Entered by you**, or read from a message and then **edited by you**.
- An explicitly recorded percentage bonus shows its rate and, when the base is known, its money equivalent. Older free-text bonus conditions stay as written with **No amount stated · value uncertain**; the app does not infer a percentage from that text.
- A conditional bonus is still conditional. Equity wording is not a confirmed cash value.
- Totals are not added together. Read the original documents before treating several figures as one package.

The comparison itself uses saved data and makes no AI request.

## Check what matters to you

**Your requirements** uses preferences from **Career → Knowledge → Preferences**, such as minimum base salary, locations and maximum office days. Keep them current before comparing.

A higher salary may still come with more office days than you want. A remote offer may leave its working hours or travel expectations unstated. Write down what would change your choice.

## Ask about missing terms

Under **Not in either offer yet**, read the suggested questions and who should answer them. Use **Copy questions** to take them to your own email or notes.

Copied questions include their intended recipients. Offer selections stay with the coaching conversation in this browser when you return through Recent; review the context chip before sending another message.

Choose **Plan what to ask** to open the coach with both offers as context. Review its context and privacy line before sending. You can ask for a conversation plan or a negotiation draft; you decide what to send and which offer to choose.

If updated terms arrive, save another offer version and compare that version. The comparison does not replace the original offer documents or record an acceptance on your behalf.

From an Offer-stage workspace, **Compare offers** opens that application's newest saved terms. The action stays unavailable while its saved offers load or a read fails; use **Try again** to retry a failed read. Your saved offer versions stay unchanged.
