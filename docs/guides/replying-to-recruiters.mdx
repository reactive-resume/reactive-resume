---
title: "Reply to a recruiter"
description: "Read an employer message, edit a reply and review proposed changes to your application and follow-up reminder."
---

A recruiter asks for your availability and a project example. **Messages** helps you understand the request, draft a reply and update the next step for that job.

You paste the message yourself and send the reply from your own email. There is no mailbox connection required.

The pasted employer message saves after a short pause, on blur and when leaving. Unsaved text is kept on this browser/device. After an interrupted save, review the recovered draft before saving it over the current version, or choose **Use saved version**. No AI request is made by autosave or recovery.

## Read the message

1. Open the correct application's workspace and choose **Messages**.
2. Paste the employer's message. Remove unrelated history or private details you do not need to share.
3. Read the line naming what goes to your AI connection.
4. Select **Read it for me**.

A pasted email may include its opening From, To, Date and Subject headers; the reader keeps the current message body and drops those headers and later quoted history. Review the pasted text yourself too.

The result identifies what they ask for and what remains unconfirmed. For example, an invitation to “next week” is not a confirmed interview time.

## Edit and send the reply

Read **Suggested reply · edit before sending**. Replace suggested availability with times that actually work for you, check any project claims, and adjust the tone.

Unfinished messages and edited replies stay in this browser tab through navigation and refresh. Opening a Saved review keeps its original generated reply read-only.

Select **Copy reply**, paste it into your email, review it there and send it yourself. Copying does not send anything. Keep the final wording in your email; do not rely on the reply editor as a sent-email archive.

## Choose which application changes to apply

Under **Proposed changes**, review each selected item:

- A **stage** change can reflect an explicit move to the next round.
- A **next step or follow-up** can record what you need to do and set the application's follow-up date. The date shows with the application's next step; it does not add a reminder to Today. For a reminder in Today, add a **Remind me** schedule.
- **Offer terms** can be saved for comparison in Career → Offers.

Uncheck any change you do not want, then choose **Apply … change(s)**. The update is saved as soon as you apply it, so leaving the page right away keeps it. The notice that follows offers **Undo**, which puts back the stage and follow-up the update changed and removes the offer it saved. Undo refuses, and leaves everything as it is, when you have since changed those fields or edited that offer; change the application in Applications instead. Document versions recorded as sent stay recorded.

A row saying **Stage stays Interview** needs no change. If the application is still Saved, record its actual submission in **Apply → Mark as applied** before applying later-stage changes.

If the application changed after the review, the app rejects stale proposed changes; read the message again against the current application before retrying. This includes applying again after **Undo**.

Applying changes updates your records. It does not send the reply, confirm a time with the recruiter or accept an offer.

## Record a confirmed interview

When the employer confirms the date, time and format, add the interview through **Next step → Edit**. Include who you will meet and what to prepare. Then [make a preparation plan](/guides/preparing-for-interview-day).

## Record an offer without AI

For a written or verbal offer, choose **Add offer** at the top of **Messages**. The application is already selected. Enter its currency, pay period and known terms, then choose **Save offer**. This makes no AI request, does not send a reply, and does not change the linked resume. If the application was still at an earlier stage, saving moves it to **Offer** and the message offers **Undo**, which keeps the offer and leaves the stage where it was. The saved offer then stays in view as a one-line summary with **Compare**.

Leave unknown amounts blank; enter `0` only for a confirmed zero. Use full amounts such as `95000`, with your interface locale's separators, rather than shorthand such as `95k`. Invalid amounts stay in the form for correction.

**Respond by** is optional and uses your account timezone. Choose a valid local date and time; a time skipped by daylight saving cannot be saved. On mobile, wait for the account timezone to load or retry if loading failed. Clear the date if no deadline is known.

The offer appears in **Library** and [Career → Offers](/guides/comparing-job-offers). If you use **Read it for me** instead, review the proposed terms before applying them. Neither path accepts an offer for you.
