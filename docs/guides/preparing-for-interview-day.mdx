“My interview is at 2pm and my mind is blank.” Open the application's **Interview prep** screen. A saved briefing gives you a focus, an opening, stories to bring and questions to ask.

If you have only a few minutes, go straight to [Read the short briefing](#read-the-short-briefing). Set up a schedule beforehand when you want the next briefing ready without opening the app.

## Add the interview details

Open the application page. Choose **Edit interview** on its next-step card, or open **Details** to schedule a round. Record the date, time, timezone, duration, who you will meet and what they asked you to prepare. See [Scheduling interviews](/guides/scheduling-interviews).

Check **What you sent** too. Preparation uses recorded sent resume and letter versions when available. Without a sent version, it can use the linked current document. A linked resume is not required to start preparation.

## Make a plan before the day

1. Open **Interview prep** and choose **Plan my preparation**.
2. Read the timed plan. Open a suggested story, draft a missing one, or rehearse a question.
3. Tick completed steps. Your progress stays with the application.
4. Review **Questions to ask**. Fit questions you saved for this job appear here too.

Use **Plan again** when you need a fresh plan. Earlier generated briefings remain available in **Library**.

**Draft story** opens an editable Knowledge story with the step's title and note. Active shared facts cited by that step start checked under **Backed by**; review those choices and write the story before selecting **Save story**. Older plans can link a fact quoted verbatim in the note. Unrelated, switched-off, forgotten and application-only facts are not selected for a shared story. Opening the draft does not save it or send another AI request. The same handoff is available in the native app.

## Prepare now from Today

For an interview starting within the next 24 hours, **Today → Coming up** offers **Generate briefing now** and **Practice 3 questions**. Check the company, round time and sharing disclosure before generating. Generation uses your default AI connection and asks for approval; it saves a normal briefing without creating a preparation schedule. An earlier reminder or offer deadline does not hide these interview actions.

**Practice 3 questions** opens that round in **Practice** without an AI request. It takes up to three saved briefing or recalled questions and fills remaining places with general practice questions. Feedback still requires a separate AI action. The quick session and ordinary Practice keep separate answer drafts. If the round is no longer available, the quick link says so instead of switching to another interview.

It opens the workspace's questions; it does not copy chat text or generate new questions. Opening it works without an AI connection and preserves an unsent Coach draft. ## Have the briefing ready in the morning

1. Check your timezone in **Settings → Preferences**.
2. Open **Career → Schedules → See all → New schedule**.
3. Choose **Interview prep**, then the correct interview.
4. Choose **Morning of**, check the **AI connection**, and leave **Turn this schedule on** enabled.
5. Select **Save schedule**.

For a 14:00 interview, Morning of schedules the briefing at **08:00 in the schedule's saved timezone**. New schedules start with your account timezone. Existing schedules retain their saved timezone when edited, even if account settings change. For an interview before 10:00, it schedules two hours before the start instead. The app never turns a past time into an immediate paid run: when every briefing lead has passed, the switch says **Too close to the interview to schedule a refresh** and stays off.

You can choose **24 hours before** or **2 hours before** instead. The switch in Prepare names the first lead still ahead of the interview (24 hours before, then 2 hours before, then the morning of), and the server rejects a briefing time that has already passed when it is saved.

The browser can be closed. Delivery depends on the installation's scheduler and a working provider; a due time is not a guarantee of an exact arrival minute. When ready, the notification appears in **Today**. If it fails, check the notice and [schedule troubleshooting](/guides/career-workspace-reference#when-something-does-not-work).

A completed preparation schedule is shown under **Career → Schedules**, even if its switch remains on. It runs once for that interview; add a separate schedule for another round.

## Read the short briefing

From Today, select **Open briefing**, or use **Interview prep → Read briefing**.

Read four things:

1. **Your focus:** What to make clear in this round.
2. **Your opening:** A starting point you can put into your own words.
3. **Two stories:** Real examples to have ready.
4. **Keep it honest:** What is not supported yet and how to handle it.

## Practice one answer

Without AI, type an answer and choose **Keep this answer** to save the question and answer to this application’s notes. **Next question** continues without requesting feedback.

Open **Practice** from the plan or workspace tabs. Choose a question and select **Hear it** to have it read aloud.

Opening **Practice** from a Knowledge story keeps that story selected; with several upcoming interviews, choose which one to practice for. Typed answers and corrected transcripts stay in this browser tab through navigation and refresh. Leaving the session stops recording and speech playback.

<Frame caption="Type or speak an answer in Practice">
  <img

    alt="Lumen Practice with a simulated question, Type and Speak, feedback action and three saved attempts"

1. Choose **Speak**, then **Record your answer**. Allow the microphone if you want to use it.
2. Answer for up to two minutes, then stop.
3. Read the transcript and correct anything the transcription got wrong.
4. Select **Get feedback**.

Use **Voice settings** to choose who reads questions and who transcribes answers. Those can be different connections from the AI that reviews your answer. When no suitable speech connection is available, the app uses browser speech where supported. You can always choose **Type** instead.

## Debrief while it is fresh

After the real interview, open **Debrief**. Without AI, **Save to notes** saves the question, recalled answer and how it landed to this application’s notes. To request AI feedback:

1. Write the question they asked.
2. Record what you said as closely as you remember it.
3. Choose **Went well**, **Mixed** or **Didn't land** for how you felt it landed.
4. Select **Review my answer**.

The review separates supported claims from observations and suggests a practice priority for the next round. Recalled questions can feed later practice. Your impression of the interview is not proof of why an employer made a decision.
