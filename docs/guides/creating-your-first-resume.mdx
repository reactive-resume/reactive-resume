title: "Creating your first resume"
description: "Start a blank resume in Reactive Resume, add your name and contact details, fill in your first section, and download it as a PDF."
---

This guide takes you from an empty account to a first resume you can download. It takes a few minutes. If you already have a resume as a PDF, Word or JSON file, you can [import it](/guides/importing-resumes) instead of starting blank.

## Before you start

You need a Reactive Resume account. If you don't have one yet, see [Creating an account](/guides/creating-an-account). Sign in at [rxresu.me](https://rxresu.me), or at your own address if you host Reactive Resume yourself.

## Start a blank resume

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Documents">
    After you sign in, you land on **Today**. Select **Documents** in the sidebar, then **New document** to create a resume.

    <Frame caption="Create or import your first document">
      <img src="/images/guides/creating-your-first-resume/documents-first-visit.webp" alt="Empty Documents page with New document and Import actions" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Blank">
    Choose **Resume**, enter a **Name**, and choose **Blank** under **Start from**. Open **More options** to choose the language and paper size, then select **Create resume**. Changing the document language changes generated headings, date words and text direction; it does not translate your content. You can change it later in **Design → Page**.

    <Frame caption="Choose language and paper before creating the resume">
      <img src="/images/guides/creating-your-first-resume/new-document-language.png" alt="New document with More options open, CV language, Page size and Try with a sample resume" />
    </Frame>

    Reactive Resume creates the resume straight away and opens it in the editor. The name you entered identifies it in Documents.

  </Step>
</Steps>

On the phone website, **New document** opens a bottom sheet. Close it with **Close**, <kbd>Esc</kbd> on a connected keyboard,
or a downward swipe from its top edge. Rotating or resizing while it is open keeps your current choices.

</Frame>

  Want to look around before typing anything? Choose **More options → Try with a sample resume** to get a filled-in
  resume you can explore and delete later.
</Tip>

## Add your name and contact details

The editor opens in **Write** mode. **Full name** starts with your account name; change it if your resume should show something else. The page on the right updates as you type, and every change saves on its own. The editor bar shows **Saved** under the document name.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill in the basics">
    Open **Basics** and check your **Full name**, then add a **Headline** (for example, the job title you're aiming for), your **Email**, **Phone** and **Location**. Add a **Website** if you have one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the document name">
    The resume keeps the **Name** you entered before creating it. To pick your own name, open the document name menu at the top left and choose **Rename…**, or rename it from Documents.
  </Step>
</Steps>

</Frame>

## Add your first section

In the Write outline, under **Sections**, Reactive Resume suggests the sections most resumes have: Experience, Education, Projects, Skills and Summary. No work experience yet? Start with Education or Projects: when Experience and Education sit in the main column of the same page, they print in the order you start them. A blank resume also starts with your account's name and email filled in (a guest's has the name only). Blank resumes start with Summary, Experience, Education and Projects in that order; Profiles sits after the other main-column sections. Sample resumes keep their own order. Existing resumes and imported layouts keep their saved order.

<Frame caption="Starter sections on a new resume">
  <img
    src="/images/guides/creating-your-first-resume/starter-sections.webp"
    alt="New resume with Experience, Education, Skills and Summary suggestions, an Import it link and Add section"
  />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a section">
    Select **Experience** (or any other suggestion). For anything else, select **Add section**.
  </Step>

<Step title="Fill in the entry">
  The section opens with an empty entry. Fill in **Position** and **Company**, then **Location** and **Dates**. Turn on
  **Present** if you still work there. An experience entry appears on the page once it has a company.
</Step>

  <Step title="Add more">
    Use the back button to return to Experience, then select **Add entry** for your next job, or add more sections the same way.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Open one entry to edit its fields">
  <img

    alt="Northwind Labs experience entry for fictional Alex Morgan, with Position, Company, Location and Dates"
  />
</Frame>

## Download your resume

When you're happy with it, select **Download PDF** in the top right corner of the editor. The arrow next to it opens other formats.

<Frame caption="Download PDF and the arrow for other formats">
  <img
    src="/images/guides/creating-your-first-resume/download-pdf-button.webp"
    alt="Editor bar with modes, Undo, Redo, History, Assistant and split Download PDF button"
  />
</Frame>

To come back later, select the arrow at the top left of the editor to return to Documents, where your resume now has its own card.
