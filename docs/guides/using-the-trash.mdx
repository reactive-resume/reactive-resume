---
title: "Using the Trash"
description: "Move resumes and cover letters to Trash in Reactive Resume, undo it, restore them within 30 days, or delete them for good."
---

When you remove a resume or cover letter, it goes to **Trash** instead of disappearing. It stays there for 30 days, so you can bring it back if you change your mind.

## Move a document to Trash

On Documents, open the document's **⋯** menu and choose **Move to Trash**. In the editor, open the document name menu at the top left and choose **Move to Trash**; you return to Documents.

Reactive Resume doesn't ask you to confirm, because the move can be undone. A message appears at the bottom of the screen. Select **Undo** to put the document back straight away.

While a resume is in Trash, its public link stops working. Restoring it brings the resume back as it was.

<Note>Locked documents can't be moved to Trash. Choose **Unlock** in the document's menu first.</Note>

## Open the Trash

Once Trash holds something, a **Trash** item with a count appears at the bottom of the sidebar. Select it to open the Trash page.

<Frame caption="Trash in the sidebar">
  <img
    src="/images/guides/using-the-trash/trash-in-sidebar.webp"
    alt="The Trash item in the sidebar with a count of 1"
  />
</Frame>

You can also open the [command bar](/guides/using-the-command-bar) with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows and Linux) and choose **Trash**, or go to `https://rxresu.me/dashboard/trash`. Self-hosters use their own address.

The Trash page lists each document with its **Type**, linked **Application**, and how many days are left under **Deleted in**. It loads up to 100 documents at a time; use **Previous** and **Next** below the rows to browse more. The sidebar count covers all documents still within the 30-day retention period. When it's empty, it says **Trash is empty**.

Previous rows stay visible while another page loads, but selection and actions wait until the requested page arrives. If loading fails, choose **Try again**; the previous rows remain visible and unavailable for changes.

<Frame caption="The last page of Trash after restoring one document">
  <img
    src="/images/guides/using-the-trash/trash-pagination.png"
    alt="Trash lists four archived letters on its last page, with selection checkboxes, row options, Empty trash and pagination showing 201–204 of 204"
  />
</Frame>

Select a row's checkbox to act on one document, or select several. **Select this page** chooses the rows currently loaded on the page. The bar at the bottom offers **Restore** and **Delete permanently…** for that selection. **Empty trash…** in the page header is separate: it deletes everything in Trash, not only the rows you selected.

## Restore a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the documents">
    Tick the checkbox beside each document you want back, or select **Select this page** for the loaded rows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Restore">
    Select **Restore**. Restoring asks for no confirmation. The documents go back to Documents with their content, tags
    and links intact.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can still restore one document from its row menu. On the Trash page, select **⋯** at the end of the document's row, then choose **Restore**.

## Delete a document for good

Documents are deleted permanently 30 days after you moved them to Trash. To delete them sooner:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select what to delete">
    Tick the checkbox beside each document, or use **Select this page** for the loaded rows. To delete everything in Trash instead, choose **Empty trash…** in the page header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Select **Delete permanently…** or **Empty trash…**, then hold **Hold to delete** to confirm. Choose **Keep in Trash** to change your mind.

    <Frame caption="Confirming a permanent delete">
      <img src="/images/guides/using-the-trash/delete-now-confirmation.webp" alt="A dialog asking Delete Product Designer forever? It will be deleted for good and can't be restored, with Keep in Trash and Hold to delete buttons" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The confirmation names the selected rows for a selection, and says **Empty Trash?** with the total count for **Empty
  trash…**. The distinction matters when only some rows are loaded: Empty trash always covers the whole Trash.
</Note>

<Frame caption="Empty Trash covers every document in Trash">
  <img
    src="/images/guides/using-the-trash/empty-trash-confirmation.webp"
    alt="A dialog asking Empty Trash? All 3 documents in Trash will be deleted for good and can't be restored, with Keep in Trash and Hold to delete buttons"
  />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  A deleted document can't be restored, and neither can its version history. If you might need it again, download a JSON
  copy first (see [Exporting your resume](/guides/exporting-your-resume)).
</Warning>
