Your profile is the name, photo, username and email tied to your account. You can change any of them in **Settings → Account**. This page also covers changing your password.

<Note>
  Your profile is separate from your resumes. Changing your account name or photo does not change the name or photo on
  any resume. Those live in each resume's **Basics** section.
</Note>

## Open your profile

Select your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Settings**. **Account** opens first, with **Profile** at the top.

On a phone, **Settings** opens a short list first. Tap **Account**.

## Change your name, username or email

Click a field, type the new value, then press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or click anywhere else. The change saves as soon as you leave the field. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> before leaving to undo what you typed.

If a value can't be saved, it stays in the field with the reason underneath, for example when a username is already taken.

- **Name**: up to 64 characters. Shown in the app, not on your resumes.
- **Username**: 3 to 64 characters, using lowercase letters, numbers, dots, hyphens and underscores. The field shows your site address in front of it, because the username is part of every public resume link.
- **Email**: see below. Changing it takes an extra step.

<Warning>
  Changing your username changes the address of every public resume. Links you have already shared, such as the one on a
  job application, stop working. Share the new links after the change. Your old username stays reserved for you for 30
  days, so nobody else can take it and its links in that time.
</Warning>

### Confirm a new email address

When you change **Email** and your current address is verified, Reactive Resume first sends an approval link to the current address, so nobody else can move your account. Open it, then open the confirmation link that arrives at the new address. Your account keeps the old address until both are done. Check both inboxes, including spam.

If your current address isn't verified, the confirmation link goes straight to the new address.

If your current address isn't verified yet, the hint under the field offers **Not verified yet. Resend the link**. On self-hosted copies without email delivery, the hint says the address can't be verified instead.

## Change your photo

Select **Change photo** and choose an image. It uploads right away and replaces your initials across the app. Images up to 10 MB work; the site usually shrinks them to fit 800 × 800 pixels. Select **Remove photo** to go back to your initials.

## Change your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the password dialog">
    In **Sign-in & security**, next to **Password**, select **Change**.
  </Step>

<Step title="Enter your current and new password">
  Fill in **Current password** and **New password**. The new password must be different from the current one and at
  least 8 characters long. Select **Sign out of all other devices** to revoke your other sessions when changing it.
</Step>

  <Step title="Select Change password">
    A message confirms the change. You stay signed in on this device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you signed up with a social account, the **Password** row reads **You sign in without one**, and the button says **Set a password**. It takes you to the password reset page, where you can have a link emailed to you to create one.
