title: "Managing your documents"
description: "Find, sort, rename, duplicate, lock and remove your resumes and cover letters from the Documents page in Reactive Resume."
---

**Documents** is the page you see after signing in. It holds every resume and cover letter in your account, so you can keep one version per role, company or language and find the right one fast.

<Frame caption="Search, filter and organize documents">
  <img
    src="/images/guides/managing-documents/documents-grid.webp"
    alt="Documents with three fictional resumes, current toolbar, tag filters and grid controls"
  />
</Frame>

Each card shows the document's first page, its name, its type and when you last edited it. A card made on this device that you haven't opened yet has a **New** badge, and a locked document shows a lock icon. A resume with a public link shows a globe icon, or a password icon (**Public, with a password**) when visitors need a password to open it; the Android and iPhone apps show the same words under the name. If a document is linked to a job application, the company name appears under it. A letter that isn't linked shows who it's to once you fill in the recipient's company or name.

"Edited" means a change to the content: a resume's sections, wording or design, or a letter's text, recipient or design. Renaming, tags, sharing, locking and moving to Trash and back don't change it, so **Last edited** order stays put while you tidy up.

## Open a document

Select a card (or a name in list view) to open it. Resumes open in the resume editor and cover letters in the letter editor. You can also open the card's **⋯** menu and choose **Open**.

## Find a document

Use the controls above your documents:

<Frame caption="Search, filter and organize documents">
  <img
    src="/images/guides/managing-documents/documents-toolbar.webp"
    alt="Documents with three fictional resumes, current toolbar, tag filters and grid controls"
  />
</Frame>

- **All**, **Resumes** and **Letters** show one type at a time. Tab counts cover every result matching your search, tags and Public filter, across all pages. A job's resume and letter stay together under the job's heading.
- **Search** matches document names, tags, and the company or role of a linked application. It accepts up to 500 characters. Press <kbd>/</kbd> anywhere on the page to jump to it. In the search box, <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens the first result, even if you press it before the results update; <kbd>↓</kbd> moves to the results; <kbd>Esc</kbd> clears the search, and a second <kbd>Esc</kbd> leaves the box.
- The sort menu orders documents by **Last edited** (the default), **Name** or **Created**. **Last edited** follows content edits only.
- Tag chips appear once any document has a tag. See [Organizing documents with tags](/guides/organizing-with-tags).
- **Public** shows resumes with their public link on. Combine it with search and tags to find the documents you're sharing.

Documents loads up to 100 items at a time. Use **Previous** and **Next** below the results to move between pages. Changing a search, filter or sort starts at the first page. **Select this page** selects only the documents shown on the current page, including the open groups below.

## Documents grouped by job

When a resume or letter is linked to a job application, Documents puts every document for that job under one heading with the company and role. A job's resume and letter sit together, even when other documents were edited more recently. Documents without a job stay in the library's normal order.

Select a job's heading to collapse or open its documents. A **Closed** job starts collapsed, so finished applications take less room while their documents stay available. **All**, **Resumes**, **Letters**, search, tags and **Public** still filter the documents inside the groups, and the page still shows up to 100 documents at a time. A group can continue on the next page when its documents fall across a page boundary. The Android and iPhone apps group documents the same way; Trash stays a flat list.

While another page or search loads, the previous results stay visible. Selection and document actions wait for the new results. If loading fails, the search and filters stay available; change them or choose **Try again**. Counts and tags continue to describe the last successful results.

Changing the search, filters, sort or page cancels an <kbd>Enter</kbd> pressed while results were loading. Press

<kbd>Enter</kbd> again to open the first result from the new request.

Tag chips show the first 100 tags alphabetically. Search for a tag to find further tags and matching documents.

If nothing matches, select **Clear search and filters** to see everything again. A **Resumes** or **Letters** tab with nothing of its kind yet says so instead, with **New resume** or **New cover letter** to start one.

<Tip>
  The page address keeps your tab, search, tags and sort, so you can bookmark a filtered view. To search across
  documents and applications from anywhere in the app, use the [command bar](/guides/using-the-command-bar).
</Tip>

## Use the document menu

Every card and row has a **⋯** menu. You can also right-click a card or row (or press and hold on a touch screen) to open the same menu.

<Frame caption="Actions for a resume in Documents">
  <img
    src="/images/guides/managing-documents/document-options-menu.webp"
    alt="The Game Developer Resume options menu in Documents: Open, Share, Download PDF, Copy public link, Select, Rename, Duplicate, Copy for a job, Tags, Lock editing and Move to Trash"
  />
</Frame>

| Item                          | What it does                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Open**                      | Opens the document in its editor.                                                                                                                   |
| **Share…**                    | Resumes only. Opens the editor's Share view: public link, password and web page.                                                                    |
| **Copy public link**          | Resumes with their public link on. Copies the link.                                                                                                 |
| **Download PDF**              | Downloads the document's current version as a PDF.                                                                                                  |
| **Select**                    | Starts a selection with this document, for actions on several at once.                                                                              |
| **Rename**                    | Lets you edit the name in place.                                                                                                                    |
| **Duplicate**                 | Asks for a name, suggesting an available numbered name, and copies the document and tags.                                                           |
| **Copy for a job…**           | Resumes only. Makes a copy linked to a job application.                                                                                             |
| **Link to application…**      | Letters only. Links the letter to one of your open applications, or unlinks it. **Add a job…** saves a new one from a link or posting and links it. |
| **Tags…**                     | Adds or removes tags.                                                                                                                               |
| **Lock editing** / **Unlock** | Protects the document from changes, or allows them again.                                                                                           |
| **Move to Trash**             | Moves the document to Trash, where you can restore it for 30 days.                                                                                  |

**Duplicate** suggests names such as “Fictional portfolio 2” and “Fictional portfolio 3”, checking your whole library, including Trash. Copying a numbered copy continues the same sequence while its base document is present. You can change the suggested name before saving; **Cancel** creates nothing. Names can contain up to 100 characters. The Android and iPhone apps offer the same naming step.

After duplication, choose **Rename** in the confirmation to edit the new copy. On the web this brings the copy into view and selects its name for inline editing; on Android and iPhone it opens the copy's Rename sheet. The original document keeps its name and content.

## Act on several documents

Tick a document's checkbox (in list view, or on a card's top corner; on touch screens the box is always shown), or choose **Select** from its menu. A bar appears at the bottom of the page, above the tab bar on phones, with **Tags…**, **Sharing** and **Move to Trash**. The page leaves room to scroll the final row above the bar. **Sharing** can lock or unlock documents; public and private links apply to resumes only. **Select this page** adds the rest of the page; the **×** or <kbd>Esc</kbd> clears the selection.

## Rename a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Rename">
    Open the document's **⋯** menu and choose **Rename**. The name turns into a text field with the current name selected.

    <Frame caption="Rename a document in the library">
      <img src="/images/guides/managing-documents/rename-inline.webp" alt="Document name being edited inline in Documents" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the new name">
    Names can be up to 100 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or cancel">
    Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or click elsewhere to save. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to keep the old name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A new blank resume takes its name from its headline until you rename it. After you rename it, the name stays as you typed it.

In the web resume editor, select the document name in the top bar, then **Rename…**. The name becomes an inline field: <kbd>Enter</kbd> or leaving the field saves, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels. A failed save keeps the entered name open for retry. On Android and iPhone, **Rename…** opens the same naming sheet used by the library.

The resume editor's document menu also offers **Tags…** and **Copy for a job…**. Tag changes keep the resume's content intact; copying for a job saves pending edits before creating the linked copy.

<Frame caption="Organize the open resume from its document menu">
  <img
    src="/images/guides/career/builder-document-actions.png"
    alt="Resume document menu with Rename…, Duplicate, Copy for a job…, Tags…, Lock editing, Notes, Details, Print, More download formats, History and Move to Trash"

For a new resume with an automatically generated address, renaming it here or in the editor also updates its address while
it has never been published. No redirect is kept for that private address. An address you chose yourself, an address from
a previously published resume, and older saved addresses stay unchanged. If the new address is taken, a number is added.
See [Sharing your resume as a web page](/guides/sharing-your-resume-publicly#change-the-address) to change a published address.

## Duplicate a document

Choose **Duplicate** to make a separate copy. It suggests an available numbered name that you can edit, and keeps the original's tags. The copy appears at the top of the list with a **New** badge. Editing the copy never changes the original.

Duplicating is useful when you want a short and a long version, a version in another language, or a safe place to try a new template.

## Copy a resume for a job

**Copy for a job…** opens the **Copy a resume for a job** dialog with that resume under **Start from**; select **Change** to pick another. Pick an application under **For which job?** (each shows its role and company), or **No job yet**. Check the suggested **Name**, and select **Create and open**. For a new job, enter its details as described below. **Cancel** closes the dialog.

When the dialog opens from **New** or a saved job, **Start from** picks your base resume: the most recently edited resume that isn't already a copy for a job. Each resume shows the job it was made for (**Copy for _company_**) or **Not linked to a job**, so a copy tailored for one job isn't used as the start for another by accident. When every resume is a job copy, the most recent one is picked.

If the job isn't saved yet, select **Add a job**, enter its **Company** and **Role** (and the posting, if you have it), then **Save job, create and open**. The job is saved to Applications as a saved job together with the copy linked to it: if either can't be saved, neither is, and trying again doesn't save them twice. No AI request is made.

  <img

When you pick an application, the copy is linked to it and Check uses that job's posting. The original stays unchanged. The copy opens for manual editing; if you already have a usable AI connection, the assistant also opens with the job's context. For a saved job, this copy becomes its prepared resume. For the full workflow, see [Tailoring a resume for a job](/guides/tailoring-a-resume-for-a-job).

## Lock a document

Choose **Lock editing** to protect a document you've already sent, so it can't change by accident. While a document is locked:

- its card shows a lock icon;
- **Rename**, **Tags…**, **Link to application…** and **Move to Trash** are unavailable;
- the editor shows it read-only. In the resume editor a note reads "Locked. Unlock to edit." with an **Unlock** button.

You can still open, duplicate and download it. Choose **Unlock** in the same menu to edit it again.

## Remove a document

Choose **Move to Trash**. After the move succeeds, the document briefly fades out and a message appears with **Undo**. Restoring from Trash uses the same brief fade there. Search, filter, page and navigation changes do not play removal fades. Documents stay in Trash for 30 days before they're deleted for good. See [Using the Trash](/guides/using-the-trash).

After you remove the last document, the library shows **No documents** and **New document**, with a Trash count when documents can be restored. It does not repeat the first-visit introduction.

## Create a new document

Select **New** in the sidebar, or press <kbd>N</kbd> anywhere outside a text field, to open the **New** dialog. On Applications, New opens **Save job** instead; on the Letters tab, it leads with a new letter.

On the phone website, **New** opens from the bottom. Its **Close** button, <kbd>Esc</kbd> and a downward swipe from the top edge dismiss it. Resizing an open form preserves its current choices; closing and reopening starts a new form.

<Frame caption="One New dialog for resumes, letters and saved jobs">
  <img
    src="/images/guides/managing-documents/new-document-dialog.webp"
    alt="New dialog offering Import a resume, Copy a resume for a job, Start blank, New cover letter instead, Save a job instead and Try with a sample resume"
  />
</Frame>

- **Import a resume**: build a resume from a PDF, Word, JSON or LinkedIn file. See [Importing a resume](/guides/importing-resumes).
- **Copy a resume for a job**: copy one of your resumes, as described above.
- **Start blank**: create an empty resume and open it. See [Creating your first resume](/guides/creating-your-first-resume).
- **New cover letter instead**: create a letter. It takes your details and design from the resume you edited most recently. While you have an "Untitled letter", the next is "Untitled letter 2", and so on. See [Writing a cover letter](/guides/writing-a-cover-letter).
- **Try with a sample resume**: create a filled-in example resume to explore.

You can also drop a file anywhere on the Documents page to import it. On an empty library, **Choose a file** opens your file picker straight away.

When preparing a saved job with no existing resume, select **Import or create a resume**, then import a file or select **Start blank**. That resume is linked to the saved job and keeps its posting context. No AI connection is required to create, copy or edit a resume manually.
