[Browse the free resume template gallery](https://rxresu.me/resume-templates) to compare all 18 layouts and download sample PDFs before creating an account.

A template decides the layout of your resume: where the header sits, whether there is a sidebar, and how headings and entries look. You can switch templates at any time. Your content, fonts and colors stay the same.

## Open the template gallery

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your resume">From **Documents**, open the resume you want to change.</Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Design">
    Select **Design** at the top of the editor, or press <kbd>2</kbd> while you are not typing in a field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Template group">
    **Template** is the first group in the Design panel. Use the row of links at the top of the panel (**Template**,
    **Entries**, **Type**, **Color**, **Page**, **Advanced**) to jump between groups.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each thumbnail shows the first page of **your** resume in that template, so you can judge it with your real content. While the thumbnails are being drawn, you briefly see a sample image instead.

## Filter the gallery

The chips above the thumbnails narrow the list:

| Filter          | Shows                                                                                                    |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **All**         | All 18 templates.                                                                                        |
| **One column**  | Templates that read top to bottom in a single column.                                                    |
| **Two columns** | Templates with a separate sidebar column.                                                                |
| **ATS-safe**    | Templates with one reading order and plain text headings, which applicant tracking systems read cleanly. |

The counter on the right (for example "7 of 18 shown") tells you how many templates match.

## Preview and apply a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Preview it on the page">
    Hover over a thumbnail, or move to it with <kbd>Tab</kbd>. The page on the right redraws in that template, and a
    label above it reads "Previewing *name* · click to apply". Nothing is saved yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply it">
    Click the thumbnail. The template is applied and a message confirms the change, with an **Undo** button if you
    change your mind.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or go back">
    Move the pointer away from the thumbnails, or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, to return to your current template.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Photo side** column says where your profile picture sits in the header when you upload one. "—" means the template draws no header picture. On right-to-left resumes the picture and the name swap sides.

In a one-column template, sections you placed in the sidebar print after the main sections instead of beside them. Bronzor prints them as labelled rows. On two-column templates you can move the sidebar to the other side; see [Arranging the layout](/guides/arranging-the-layout).

In a single-column header, Onyx and Lapras are the templates that put the picture on the left, with your name and headline beside it on the right. The others either put it on the right (Meowth, Rhyhorn, Scizor, Smeargle, Porygon), centre it above the header (Bronzor, Kakuna), or show no picture (Persian).

## Tips for choosing

- **Match Persian's preview.** In **Advanced → Typography**, set the heading font to **Latin Modern Roman Caps** with **Regular (400)** weight and the body font to **Computer Modern Serif**, and choose **Letter** paper. The name and section headings both use the heading font.
- **Applying through job portals?** Start with the **ATS-safe** filter. Two-column layouts can be read out of order by applicant tracking systems, and Check mode warns you when sidebar sections might be affected. See [Checking your resume](/guides/checking-your-resume).
- **Lots of experience?** Compare how full each thumbnail's first page looks. After you apply a template, the page count in the zoom bar at the bottom of the page tells you the total.
- **Judge with real content.** Because the gallery draws your own resume, fill in your details first and then pick.

## Change how entries look

The **Entries** group, below **Template**, arranges the header of each Experience, Education and Volunteer entry: its layout, whether the company or the position comes first, and an accent. These settings work with every template and stay when you switch templates. See [Changing how entries look](/guides/changing-how-entries-look).

## Reset the design settings

If you have changed a lot of design settings and want a clean slate, open **Advanced** at the bottom of the Design panel and select **Reset design defaults**. On a phone, and in the Android and iPhone apps, **Advanced** is at the bottom of the design sheet's **Page** tab. This resets fonts, text size, line height, colors, level style, the **Entries** settings, sidebar width, margins, spacing and the icon and underline switches. Your template, paper size, language, date format, section placement and custom CSS stay as they are. A message offers **Undo**.

The reset doesn't depend on the template: every template goes back to the same standard settings (IBM Plex Serif at 10 pt, a red accent, circle levels, stacked entry headers and hidden section icons). Pick a font pairing and accent color again afterwards if you want a different starting point.

<Note>
  If Design's controls are greyed out, the resume is locked. Open the document name menu at the top left and select
  **Unlock editing**.
</Note>
