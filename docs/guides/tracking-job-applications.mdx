**Applications** is where you keep track of the jobs you're going for. Each application holds the role, the company, the saved job posting, the resume and letter you sent, your contacts, notes and a dated history. It also works out what each application needs next, such as an interview coming up or a follow-up that's due.

For focused work on one job, open its details and select **Open workspace**. [Career and Application Workspaces](/guides/career-overview) explains Fit, Apply, interview preparation, practice, replies and saved results. Shared experience and search schedules live under **Career**.

To open it, select **Applications** in the sidebar (on a phone, it's in the bottom tab bar).

## How stages work

Every application sits in one stage. The first five stages form a pipeline you move through in order:

| Stage         | Use it when                                                              |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Saved**     | You found the job but haven't applied yet.                               |
| **Applied**   | You sent your application.                                               |
| **Screening** | A recruiter or hiring manager got back to you, or you have a first call. |
| **Interview** | You're in the interview process.                                         |
| **Offer**     | You received an offer.                                                   |
| **Closed**    | The application ended, whatever the outcome.                             |

You can rename, recolor and reorder stages, and add your own, such as **Take-home assignment** or **Ghosted**. Each one you add counts as one of these stages. See [Customizing application stages](/guides/customizing-application-stages).

You can close an application from any stage. Closing asks for a reason (**Accepted offer**, **Not selected**, **I withdrew**, **Accepted another offer** or **No response**), which Insights uses later. Closing from **Offer** starts on **Accepted offer**. Closed applications are hidden until you turn on **Show closed applications**. See [Managing an application](/guides/managing-an-application#closing-an-application).

<Note>
  When an application with a linked resume or letter reaches **Applied** or a later stage, Reactive Resume saves a copy
  of each as a "Sent to *company*" version. You can always open exactly what you sent, even after you keep editing the
  document.
</Note>

## Choosing a view

In the web app, use the tabs above the list to switch views. Your choice is kept in the page address, so a bookmark opens the same view. In the native mobile app, open **Applications** and use the view selector below the heading: **List**, **Board**, **Calendar** or **Insights**. All four views are available on phones and tablets.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="List">
    The default view. Categories that usually need you first appear first: **Interview**, **Offer**, **Screening**, **Applied**, **Saved**, then **Closed** when shown. Custom stages within a category follow your configured stage order.

    - Select a group heading to collapse or expand it.
    - Within each group, rows follow **Next step**: dated interviews, offer deadlines and follow-ups first, soonest first, then the application that has waited longest in its stage. The wait counts from the stage's own date, such as the stage date in an imported CSV, not from when the row was added or last changed. An imported stage without a date has no elapsed wait and sorts after known stage dates. Moving between custom lanes in the same stage does not restart that clock. Web and native use the same ordering; equal dates keep a stable order.
    - Select the **Role**, **Next step** or **Updated** column heading to sort within each group. Select it again to reverse the order.
    - The **Next step** column shows the next interview, the follow-up you set, or what the stage is waiting on: how long ago you applied, **Waiting to hear back** after screening, **Waiting for interview feedback** once your interviews are done, **No interview scheduled** in Interview without an interview date, or **Reply to the offer**, **Add the offer's terms** or **Decide on the offer** in Offer, depending on the saved terms and deadline. It turns amber when something is overdue, when you've had no reply for 10 days or more, and for an offer to decide.
    - The **Sent** column shows an icon for a linked resume and a linked letter. It says **No documents attached** in amber when an application was sent without a linked document.
    - Select a row to open its details.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Board">
    One column per stage, with a card for each application. Drag a card to another column to move it to that stage. Each card also has a **⋯** menu with **Move to…**, **Mark as closed…** and **Delete…**, which is handy if you prefer the keyboard. When **Show closed applications** is on, a **Closed** column appears too. Dragging a card there asks why it ended before closing it, so Insights can use the outcome. In the mobile app, the **Closed** column is always the last column on the board.

    At a 1440px desktop browser width, the five standard open stages fit together, including **Offer**. Native tablet lanes also adapt to the available board width. Narrower boards and boards with extra stages keep horizontal scrolling so cards stay readable.

  <Tab title="Insights">
    Charts about how your applications are going: how far they get, how often and how quickly people reply, and where your applications come from. See [Viewing application insights](/guides/viewing-application-insights).

In a narrow phone browser, the web page shows **List**, **Insights** and **Calendar**, and list rows show the company and next step on a second line. **Board** remains available on wider browser layouts. The native mobile app offers all four views on phones and tablets.

## Keyboard triage in List

In the web app, use <kbd>Tab</kbd> to focus an application's role button. Its outline shows which application receives shortcuts. Moving focus outside the row stops bare-key triage. In the native app with a hardware keyboard, choose **Keyboard triage** in List; the outlined row and its role/company identify the current application. Choose **Stop keyboard triage** to turn it off.

| Key                         | Action                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <kbd>j</kbd> / <kbd>k</kbd> | Focus the next / previous displayed application. Stops at the ends; follows sorting, filters and expanded groups.                                                                                    |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd>            | Open the focused application's details.                                                                                                                                                              |
| <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>5</kbd>   | Move to **Saved**, **Applied**, **Screening**, **Interview** or **Offer**, respectively, using that category's default stage. **Undo** cancels the pending move; a newer stage saved elsewhere wins. |
| <kbd>x</kbd>                | Toggle the focused application in the existing bulk selection.                                                                                                                                       |
| <kbd>f</kbd>                | Record **Followed up** for an open application beyond Saved. Sends no message; see [Managing an application](/guides/managing-an-application).                                                       |

Typing in a field or editor, composing text, modifier chords, and open dialogs, menus or sheets do not trigger background triage. Holding a command key does not repeat stage, selection or contact actions. A hidden or removed application cannot receive the next action.

Native bare-key triage requires an updated iOS or Android store build. Older installed binaries retain their existing controls and modifier shortcuts.

## Finding applications

Type in **Search applications** to filter the list as you type. Search looks at the role, company, location, contact names and tags. If nothing matches, select **Clear search**.

In the web app, press <kbd>/</kbd> with an Applications view focused, outside a text field, to focus search. In the native mobile app, use <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd> on an iPad hardware keyboard or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd> on Android. Open sheets and alerts keep keyboard focus.

In the web app, **Show closed applications** adds closed applications to the List, Board and Calendar views. The native mobile calendar shows only open applications. Insights always counts every application.

Rows and board cards show up to two of an application's tags, with a count for the rest. Select a tag to search for it without leaving the list; the search box shows the tag, and **Clear search** returns to every application.

## Acting on several applications at once

In the List view on a computer or tablet, select the checkboxes next to the applications you want. The checkbox in the table header selects every row in the expanded stages, or clears them; it shows a partial state while only some are selected. After you select at least one row, a bar appears at the bottom of the page with:

- **Select all** to add every matching application in expanded stages
- **Tags…** to add the same tag to each
- **Move to…** to move them all to one stage
- **Close…** to close them all with one reason
- **Clear selection** (×) to deselect
- **Delete permanently…** to delete them permanently (you're asked to confirm)

  <img
    src="/images/guides/tracking-job-applications/list-bulk-actions-bar.png"

  />
</Frame>

Moves and closes show at once with **Undo** in the message. They're saved when the message goes away, or when you leave the page; **Undo** puts the selected applications back where they were, without recording a stage change. If you move one of them again before then, here or on another device, that later move stays, even if it moved away and back to the same stage: the bulk move leaves it and says so, and **Undo** leaves it too. Other edits, such as a note, don't stop the move. If you move the same applications again before the first move is saved, or while a move of one of them from its own menu or details, **Mark as applied**, or a message's changes are still being saved, the later move is saved after the earlier one, so it's the one that stays. Removing a stage in **Stages** moves its applications to another stage of the same kind without counting as a change. Tags and deletions are saved straight away.

You can act on up to 200 applications at once. Larger selections show a reminder; deselect rows or narrow the search before choosing an action. The list leaves room to scroll its final rows above the selection bar, including when its buttons wrap.

In the native mobile app, open a row's **⋯** actions to start a selection, then tap other rows to add them. The selection controls appear above the list with **Select all**, **Tags…**, **Move to…**, **Close…**, **Clear selection** and **Delete permanently…**.

Selection follows the visible rows. Hiding closed applications, collapsing a stage, changing the search or applying **Needs you** removes hidden selections before a bulk action.

## Focus on what needs you

Select **Needs you** in Applications to show jobs whose next step needs your attention: an interview within a week, a follow-up due within a day or overdue, a long wait without a reply, or an offer to decide. The filter uses the same next-step rules as [Career → Today](/guides/career-overview), on your account's timezone. Closed applications need no action and stay out of this filter.

The filter works in List, Board, Calendar and Insights, and stays on when you switch views. Search can narrow it further. Turn **Needs you** off to show the other applications again; if no applications match, **Clear filters** removes the search and attention filters. Filtering changes what you see, without changing any saved application or stage.

Calendar shows events from matching applications. An application whose next interview is more than a week away may be hidden; turn **Needs you** off to see its events. Calendar and Insights offer **Clear filters** when the filtered results have no events or sent applications to show.

## Follow-up reminders

A reminder appears above the list when applications owe a follow-up: the follow-up date you set is due, an application has been at **Applied** or **Screening** for 10 days without a reply, or 10 days have passed since its last recorded interview ended.

- With one, the reminder names it, for example "Harborlight Labs: no reply for 17 days." Select **Open** to go to that application.
- With several, it counts them, for example "8 applications need a follow-up." Select **Show** to list only those applications, then **Show all** to return to the full list.

Select **×** to dismiss the reminder. Dismissing is remembered on this device, so those applications don't come back in it here. The same rules feed **Needs you** in [Career → Today](/guides/career-overview), which also lists interviews in the next week and offers to decide. The native mobile app shows the same reminder above its list.

## Opening Applications from anywhere

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows and Linux) and type a company or role to find an application from the first list, or choose **Applications** to go to the page. Run **Save job** to open the add dialog. See [Using the command bar](/guides/using-the-command-bar).
