Add each interview to its application so it shows as the application's next step, in its history and on the Calendar view. You can also set a follow-up reminder, and send any dated step to the calendar app you already use.

## Schedule an interview

You can start from the application or from the calendar.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From an application">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the application">
        Select it in **Applications** to open its details.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose Schedule an interview…">
        Under **Next step**, select **Edit**, then **Schedule an interview…**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Frame caption="Edit the next interview or set a follow-up">
      <img src="/images/guides/scheduling-interviews/next-step-edit-menu.webp" alt="Next-step Edit menu with Edit this interview, Schedule an interview and Set a follow-up" />
    </Frame>

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From the calendar">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Calendar view">
        In **Applications**, select the **Calendar** tab.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Start scheduling">
        Select **Schedule interview**. In the web app, you can also hover over a day and select **+** to start on that day.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose the application">
        Under **Application**, pick the application the interview belongs to. Closed applications aren't listed. In the native mobile app, select **Continue** to open the interview form.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

You can also use **Next step → Edit** in an application workspace. Then fill in the interview:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the interview type">
    Choose **Screening**, **Technical**, **Behavioral**, **Onsite** or **Other**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date, time and timezone">
    Enter **Date** and **Time**. The timezone is shown beside Time and starts from your account's setting in **Settings
    → Preferences**. If the invitation uses another timezone, select **Interview is in another timezone** and choose it.
    Check the equivalent time shown for your own timezone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the length">
    Under **Length · minutes**, choose 30, 45, 60 or 90. An existing interview with another length keeps that value
    available when you edit it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the meeting details">
    **Location** can hold a meeting link, address or phone number. Under **Who you'll meet**, use **Add a person** for
    each interviewer and add their name, role and optional public profile link. Copy the employer's request into **What
    they asked you to prepare**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">Select **Save interview**.</Step>
</Steps>

The interview appears in the application's **Activity**, on the calendar, and as the **Next step** until it has finished. Interview times in **Next step**, **Activity** and **Calendar**, including day grouping and **Today**/**Tomorrow**, use your account timezone in **Settings → Preferences**, as **Career → Today** does. Stage changes and notes keep their recorded whole-day dates. Editing an interview keeps its saved timezone, and calendar export keeps the same instant.

Scheduling an interview does not change the application's stage; move it to **Interview** when appropriate.

## Edit or delete an interview

Select the interview on the calendar, in **Upcoming interviews**, or in the application's **Activity**. You can also use **Edit → Edit this interview…** in the details sheet, or **Next step → Edit** in its workspace. Change what you need and select **Save interview**.

A date and time skipped by a daylight-saving change is rejected. Pick a valid local time instead of accepting an automatic shift.

To remove it, select **Delete**. The notice offers **Undo** for six seconds. Undo keeps the original round and its linked schedules. Removing an interview pauses its briefing schedules; rescheduling moves them to the new time. Check **Career → Today → Schedules** after a change.

## Prepare for the round

Open the application's workspace and choose **Interview prep** for a timed plan, briefing and questions to ask. You can enable a morning briefing in advance, then use **Practice** to rehearse out loud. See [Get ready for interview day](/guides/preparing-for-interview-day).

## Use the Calendar view

In the web app, the **Calendar** tab shows one month of interviews across your applications. It follows the current month in your account timezone until you browse to a month. **This month** restores that behavior.

- Use the arrows to change month, and **This month** to come back to today.
- Each day shows up to three interviews, with the time and company. Select **+_n_ more** to see the rest of a busy day.
- **Upcoming interviews**, on the right, lists every interview that hasn't finished yet, grouped by day. Select a card to edit the interview, or **View application** to open the application.
- The legend under the calendar shows the color of each interview type.

### In a phone browser

The **Calendar** tab stays available, including when you open a Calendar link directly. Instead of the month grid, **Upcoming interviews** lists every interview that hasn't finished yet, grouped by day, including appointments in later months. Month boundaries and day headings use your account timezone. Each card includes the time range, company, interview type and role.

- Use the arrows to browse one month's interviews, including past appointments. **This month** returns to the current month and the full upcoming agenda.
- Select a card to edit the interview, or **View application** to open its application details.
- Select **Schedule interview** to add another appointment.

### In the native mobile app

Open **Applications** and choose **Calendar** in the view selector below the heading. Calendar is available on phones and tablets, alongside **List**, **Board** and **Insights**.

- Use the arrows to change month, or **This month** to return to today. Until you browse or select a day, the calendar follows the current account date. **This month** resumes following it.
- Select a day to see its interviews and follow-ups. On phones, dots mark days with events and the day's agenda appears below the month. On a wide tablet layout, the agenda appears beside it.
- Select an interview or follow-up card to edit it, or **View application** to open its application details. **Coming up** lists upcoming events by day.
- Select **Schedule interview**, choose an application and select **Continue** to open the interview form.

The native calendar follows your application search and shows only open applications. It uses your account timezone to place events on their days.

## Set a follow-up reminder

A follow-up is a reminder to check in, for example to email the recruiter a week after applying.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Set a follow-up…">
    In the application's details, under **Next step**, select **Edit**, then **Set a follow-up…** (or **Change the
    follow-up…**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a date and say what to do">
    Choose a **Date** and, if you like, fill in **What to do**, such as "Email the recruiter". When you change an
    existing follow-up, the fields start empty, so enter the date and note again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">Select **Save**. To remove the reminder, select **Clear**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Set a date and a concrete follow-up action">
  <img
    src="/images/guides/scheduling-interviews/follow-up-dialog.webp"
    alt="Follow-up dialog with Date and What to do fields"

</Frame>

The follow-up shows as the next step (unless an interview is coming up first) and turns amber once the date has passed. You can also set the follow-up date and note in **Edit details…**.

## Add a step to your calendar

When the next step is an interview or a follow-up, select **Add to calendar** under it. Reactive Resume downloads an `.ics` file named after the company and date. Open it to add the event to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook or any other calendar app.

The event includes the type of step, the role and company, and the start and end time. Interviews also include where and your notes. Follow-ups are added as 30-minute events at 9:00 on the chosen day.

<Tip>
  The `.ics` file is a one-time copy. If you change the interview in Reactive Resume later, download it again and update
  the event in your calendar.
</Tip>
