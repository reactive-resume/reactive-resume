The form asks for your best project again. Your story is already in [Knowledge](/guides/building-career-knowledge); use **Apply** to turn it into an answer for this job.

Questions and answers are kept locally while you type, including before the autosave delay. Leaving or backgrounding flushes pending saves. If a save was interrupted, review recovered text before saving it over the current version, or use the saved version. Recovery never changes a recorded submission copy. Browser/device storage must be available; wait for saves to finish before clearing site data.

## Prepare the application together

In **Apply**, **Prepare drafts** puts pending resume tailoring proposals, a letter draft, and drafts for pasted form questions in one review. Link an owned resume and save a posting or requirements first. Separate questions with a blank line: up to five questions, each at most 2,000 characters. The existing manual preparation and individual editors remain available.

Choose **Prepare drafts**, then review the form before generating. One sharing dialog names the connection and actual primary/capable models, and lists the combined context: resume and posting; for answers, the letter, career preferences, application notes and contacts, and enabled Knowledge facts and stories. Context can be excerpted. Web access is off. Declining sends no AI request. Tool steps and each answer are separate request attempts, not one request for the whole button.

Review proposals with the existing **Accept**, **Edit**, and **Reject** controls. **Keep letter** saves the edited letter body; **Keep answer** appends that edited answer without replacing your other answers. **Undo** reverses only the last saved change and refuses to overwrite newer document or answer changes. The original base resume stays unchanged; tailoring uses a job copy. The letter body and answers remain unsaved until you keep them. Nothing submits an employer application or records a submission.

**Stop** cancels generation. A section that fails is listed while completed sections remain reviewable. Keep completed drafts before discarding the review and retrying. Review drafts recover in this browser tab or on the native device, scoped to the account; recovery needs available local storage. Saved assistant proposals remain in their conversation. A newer server/document revision can refuse an old recovered draft instead of overwriting it. **Discard** removes this local review, not already saved documents or answers.

## Add the employer's question

For saved jobs, open **Apply** from **Work on it**, where the preparation card names your current step and keeps the posting and selected documents with this job. You can copy a base resume, open either editor with **Edit**, and download the selected PDFs from each row's options menu without an AI connection. Complete only the letter and form answers the employer requires.

1. Open the application's workspace and choose **Apply**.
2. Select **Add a question**.
3. Paste the question, including any limit such as “Maximum 200 words.”
4. Press Enter or leave the question field to finish editing it.

Use **Question options → Edit question** to change it later. **Remove question** offers **Undo** once the removal is ready. Leaving the page or backgrounding the native app commits the pending removal. Undo reads the saved workspace before showing the question again; a question removed elsewhere stays removed. If that read fails, **Retry** checks again without recreating the answer.

Removal affects only that current draft question and answer. Other answers, recorded submission copies and first-submission review checks stay intact. If the target question changes on another device before removal commits, its newer wording is kept; review it before trying again.

## Draft from your experience

Read what the AI action sends, then choose **Draft**. The proposal shows what would change. Review the claims and choose **Accept** or **Skip**. The evidence it may use is your resume, switched-on Knowledge and your own typed draft: all three can support an answer, and it aims for most of the length the question allows. A claim it couldn't support appears under **Check before accepting**; it is kept for you to check, not deleted.

Clicking **Draft** with the question field still focused commits the typed question first and drafts on the same click.

Check the **Backed by** facts. Make sure “I” describes your contribution and “we” describes the team's work. Do not accept a result, responsibility or number you cannot support.

## Save and reuse a reviewed answer

Choose **Save for other applications** beside a written answer. Review its question and wording, select its source, and link existing confirmed Knowledge facts or stories when they support the claims. This does not create or confirm Knowledge records. No AI connection is required.

Choose a **Reuse scope**:

- **This application** keeps wording private to this application.
- **This employer** permits reuse only when the employer name matches. This is the default.
- **All employers** requires you to remove employer-specific wording and explicitly confirm reuse across employers.

Check **I reviewed this answer, its evidence and the application context**, then **Save reviewed answer**. Saving the same normalized question for the same scope reports a duplicate; open **Use a saved answer**, find the existing answer, and choose **Edit saved answer** instead.

**Use a saved answer** searches questions and answer text. Similar questions show a count of relevant reviewed answers; results prioritize similar questions without calling AI. Clear the search to browse everything. Each entry shows its employer, scope and last review time. Choose **Review and use**, adapt the wording for this application, check the review box, and choose **Use reviewed answer**. Changing wording resets review. Restricted answers cannot be reused for another employer or application; separately edit and review their scope first.

If linked facts, stories or a story's supporting facts change, become unavailable or are deleted, the library flags the answer and blocks reuse. **Edit saved answer** lets you correct wording, review current evidence, and remove unavailable references before saving a fresh review. An unchanged evidence link records your review, not independent verification of a claim.

Use **Copy** for one entry's answer text, **Export saved answers** for the library as JSON, or **Delete** to remove an entry after confirmation. Library edits and deletion leave application drafts and recorded submission copies unchanged. Account export includes these records and their evidence references in `career.json`; account deletion removes them. Application-only entries are deleted with that application; other scopes remain when the source application is deleted.

When linking a guest account to an existing account, saved answer wording is kept, and guest Knowledge facts, stories and the career profile move with it. A fact the account already has from the same source keeps the account's copy. Duplicate questions with the same scope stop linking until you resolve them; wording is never silently merged.

## Fit a word or character limit

Select the count beside any question to switch all displayed counts between **words** and **characters**. Select it again to switch back. Character counts include spaces.

Choose **Tighten** on an existing answer for a shorter proposal that keeps every fact you typed. Review it and choose **Accept** or **Skip**. When the question states a limit such as "150 words" or "300 characters", Tighten aims for it and the proposal shows its measured length against it. If the proposal is still over, the count turns into a warning; accepting it stays your explicit choice. If you need a different length, put the limit in the question before asking again.

**Accept** or **Skip**. If you need a specific length, put the limit in the question before asking again.

**Accept** saves the new answer at once and shows **Undo** after saving succeeds. Undo restores your previous answer and its facts only while the saved answer and its backing facts still match what you accepted. Later edits from another tab or device stay, as does anything you type after accepting. If you edit the answer while a draft is being written, the draft is discarded rather than replacing your edits: choose **Draft** or **Tighten** again.

The counter shows your answer's length; it does not block submission or guarantee the employer counts text the same way. Check the final text in the employer's form too.

## Copy or download your answers

**Copy answers** copies the nonempty questions and answers together. **Download** offers **Markdown (.md)** and **Plain text (.txt)**. You can also copy one answer's text directly for a single field.

## Review a packet for one employer form

**Employer-form packet** in **Apply** prepares an account-backed snapshot without AI. Select the application’s resume and optional letter first, then:

1. Enter the **Exact form URL**. Use the actual page you intend to fill.
2. Optionally select **Include reviewed profile name, email and phone**. Other private profile fields are excluded.
3. Choose saved answers. Answers outside their application/employer scope or with changed evidence need fresh review in the answer library.
4. Choose **Review packet**. Check the destination, contact details, work history, letter text, answer sources and document revisions. Review the selected resume in its editor too.
5. Check **I reviewed these fields, answers, destination and document content.**, then **Approve and create packet**.

Approval saves named resume/letter versions and renders their exact PDFs. Changes during preparation reject the packet; missing or trashed documents produce an error instead of a substitute. **Download approved PDF** retains the approved bytes even if the draft later changes. **Download reviewed packet** includes those PDFs, copied answers and provenance in a private JSON file. Keep it private; downloaded copies do not expire or refresh. Packets are limited to 2 MiB of combined PDFs; larger documents require manual downloads.

On desktop Chromium, import that JSON into the installed posting-capture helper, review its destination and versions, and explicitly approve **Fill this page** while that exact page is active. On iPhone and Android, use **Open employer form in browser**, copy your reviewed answers and attach the approved PDFs manually. Mobile autofill is not offered. Temporary packet/PDF share files use workspace cache cleanup on the next share and account/session transitions; copies saved elsewhere remain yours.

Desktop autofill is an experimental standard-control subset, tested only with fictional Greenhouse-, Lever- and Ashby-shaped forms. Current employer compatibility is not verified. Custom/framework controls, additional pages, login and captcha require manual completion. A filled report means a DOM value or file is present; it does not prove employer upload acceptance. Review every result and file yourself. Existing values remain unchanged; errors require the exact approved download, never another resume.

Unknown authorization and sensitive questions remain unanswered unless you explicitly select a reviewed answer for that exact question. Copy answers and use approved PDFs for unsupported forms. Optional **Draft** and **Tighten** remain separate AI actions with their existing consent controls; packet preparation itself makes no provider request.

## Submit and record what you sent

Use **Before you send** to review the linked resume and letter, check claims, and complete required answers. Make sure the application links to the actual document versions you intend to send. See [Managing an application](/guides/managing-an-application#seeing-what-you-sent).

Submit on the employer's website yourself. Then choose **Mark as applied** in Apply. The line under the button names the documents and answers it records. This keeps a saved copy of your written answers and the linked documents' versions, and moves the application to Applied.

The current answer text, submitted answer copy, document versions and stage change are recorded together. A failed save leaves the application unsubmitted and your draft available to retry.

Marking it applied records your submission; it does not submit a form for you. Later edits in Apply do not change the copy you kept at submission.

After submission is recorded from Apply, application details or another tracker entry point, **Before you send** shows a read-only record of your relevant manual checks at the first recorded submission. It has no unfinished count or outstanding submission step. Checks for absent documents or form answers are hidden; recording submission never checks anything for you. Later draft edits, document changes or stage moves do not replace this record, on web and native. Older submissions and saved answer versions without matching review history say **Historical review checks unavailable.** Copy and download actions appear only when written answers exist.

## Reopen a past version

After recording submission, **View recorded answers** opens the actual submission copy, even if newer generated answers or draft edits exist. **Back to current** returns to the editable workspace. A recorded submission does not confirm that an employer received it.

Open **Library**, find **Answers**, and choose **Open in Apply** to read that saved version. It is read-only. Select **Back to current** to return to your editable answers.

For a later interview, the same facts and stories can support your [opening and practice answers](/guides/preparing-for-interview-day).

## Reuse private application details

**Edit profile** beside **Form answers** opens **Settings → Application profile** on web, or **Settings → Profile** in the native app. Keep application-only contact details, links, address, country-specific authorization and sponsorship, relocation, requested salary and references there without AI. [See the profile controls](/guides/building-career-knowledge#keep-application-details-private).

Copy only the details you intend to use into an application answer. Changes to that answer stay with the application; they do not update your private profile or global Career preferences. **Mark as applied** preserves the entered answers as a snapshot, even if the profile later changes. Private fields are not automatically shared with the coach or inserted into documents.
