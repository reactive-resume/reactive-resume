You can choose how the app looks and which language its menus and buttons use. These settings change the app only. Your resumes always print on white paper, in the language set for each resume.

## Change the theme

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Preferences">
    Select your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Settings**, then **Preferences**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a theme under Appearance">
    - **Light**: a light background all the time.
    - **Dark**: a dark background all the time.
    - **System**: follows your device's light or dark setting and switches when it does. This is the default.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The change applies at once. Reactive Resume remembers it in this browser for a year, including after a browser restart. Another device or browser keeps its own theme choice.

### Other ways to switch

- Select your name at the bottom of the sidebar, point to **Theme**, and choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**.
- Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows and Linux), choose **Change theme to…**, then pick a theme.

## Change the interface language

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Preferences">
    Go to **Settings → Preferences**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a language under Language">
    Open the picker and choose from more than 50 languages. You can type to filter the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The page reloads in the new language. The choice is saved in this browser for a year, including after a browser restart. A language in the page address (`?locale=`) takes precedence.

You can also change it from your name in the sidebar (**Language**), or with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> and **Change language to…**.

Before you sign in, choose a language on the homepage: the language name in the header and footer leads to the languages section, which lists every language in its own name. Each name opens the homepage in that language, and the choice carries over when you create an account.

Account emails, such as email verification, password resets and email change approvals, use the language you last used Reactive Resume in, on the website or in the app. A new account starts in the language you signed up in. The links and how long they stay valid are the same in every language.

What you write keeps its own direction. In a right-to-left language such as Arabic, a German or English description, title or salary still reads left to right, each paragraph from its first letter, and Arabic text reads right to left inside an English interface.

The interface language doesn't translate your resume. Each resume has its own **Language** setting in the **Page** group of the editor's **Design** mode, which sets the section titles and date words on the page. See [Set the language](/guides/selecting-page-format#set-the-language).

<Tip>
  Spotted a wrong or missing translation? Select **Help translate** under the language picker to suggest a fix on
  Crowdin. See [Translations](/contributing/translations).
</Tip>

## Set your timezone

Go to **Settings → Preferences** and pick your timezone under **Timezone**. Interview times, reminders and schedules in Career use it. New accounts start in your device's timezone; existing accounts that never changed it stay on UTC, which the field shows as **UTC**. **Use this device's timezone** switches to it in one step, and the change is saved at once.

## Turn off haptic feedback

In the Android and iPhone apps, many taps, such as choosing a design option, give a small vibration. To turn this off, open **Settings → Preferences** and switch off **Haptic feedback** under **Feedback**. The setting applies to this device only. The website has no haptic setting.

## Reduce motion

Reactive Resume has no motion switch of its own. It follows your operating system's reduced-motion setting: turn that on, and the app replaces movement with simple fades. Nothing in the app animates on its own without you doing something first.
