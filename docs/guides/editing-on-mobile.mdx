You can build and edit a resume entirely from a phone or tablet. The editor has the same features as on a computer, arranged to fit a smaller screen. This page explains what changes and where to find things.

## On a phone

On a phone (any screen narrower than 640 pixels), the editor shows one thing at a time: either the page or a panel. Tabs at the bottom switch between them.

| Tab        | What it shows                                                  |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Write**  | Your details, summary, sections and entries.                   |
| **Page**   | The resume as it will print.                                   |
| **Design** | The page, with design settings in a sheet over its lower half. |
| **Check**  | Issues found in your resume, job match and writing review.     |
| **Share**  | Your web page, its address, privacy and visitor counts.        |

### The editor bar on a phone

The bar at the top keeps the back arrow, the document name (which opens the document menu) and the save status. On the right are two icons:

- **Assistant** opens the AI assistant full screen.
- **Download PDF** downloads the PDF straight away. For other formats, open the document menu and select **More download formats**.

There's no **History** button on a phone. Open the document menu and select **History** instead.

### Entries open full screen

When you open an entry in **Write**, it slides in and fills the screen, with larger fields that are easier to tap. The top of the screen names the section, for example **Experience**. Tap it to go back to the list. The bin icon (**Delete entry**) deletes the entry, and a message offers **Undo**.

When you type in a description or the summary, the formatting toolbar docks just above the keyboard. See [Formatting text](/guides/formatting-text#on-a-phone).

### Change the design

The **Design** tab keeps the page visible in the top half of the screen, so you can see each change as you make it. The settings sit in a sheet with four tabs: **Template**, **Type**, **Color** and **Page**. The **Advanced** settings are at the bottom of the **Page** tab.

To see more of the settings, tap the handle at the top of the sheet (**Raise the design sheet**). Tap it again to lower it.

### Check your resume

The **Check** tab lists the issues found in your resume. Select **Show on page** on an issue to jump to the **Page** tab: a bar at the top reads, for example, "Issue 1 of 3" with **Previous issue** and **Next issue** buttons, and a card at the bottom explains the issue and offers a fix. See [Checking your resume](/guides/checking-your-resume).

## On a tablet

On a tablet, the page fills the screen and the panel opens as a drawer over it. This layout applies to screens from 640 to 1023 pixels wide, which covers most tablets held upright. A tablet held sideways that is 1024 pixels or wider gets the same layout as a computer. You switch modes with **Write**, **Design**, **Check** and **Share** in the editor bar, as on a computer.

- **Show or hide the panel.** Select the panel button next to the back arrow (**Show panel** / **Hide panel**).
- **Tap a line on the page** to open the drawer on that entry. Tap an empty part of the page to close the drawer again; your selection stays.
- **Keep the panel beside the page.** Held sideways (landscape), a second button appears next to the panel button: **Keep the panel beside the page**. Select it to pin the panel next to the page instead of over it. Select it again to go back to the drawer.
- The assistant opens as a drawer too. **Download PDF** shows only its icon, and **History** is in the document menu.

## Install Reactive Resume as an app

You can add Reactive Resume to your home screen so it opens in a window of its own, without the browser's address bar.

- **iPhone and iPad (Safari):** open Reactive Resume, select the **Share** button in Safari, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open Reactive Resume, open Chrome's menu, then select **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
- **Computer (Chrome or Edge):** select the install icon at the right of the address bar, then **Install**.

This home-screen installation is the same website: you sign in the same way, and your documents stay in your account.

## Native iPhone app

The native Android app is available on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=me.rxresu). The App Store listing is coming soon.

The native app uses the same **Write**, **Page**, **Design** and **Check** modes. Its controls differ from the mobile website screenshots above:

- **Recover unsynced work.** **Documents** shows **Unsynced on this device** when local edits have not reached the server. Open the document there to continue, including after restarting without a connection. These edits belong to the signed-in account; sign-out warns before removing unsynced work.
- **Share a PDF.** Open **Share**, review **File name**, then choose **Share PDF**. **File format** offers other exports. **Link** controls public-link sharing, and **History** keeps saved versions separate.
- **Save a job quickly.** Enter **Company**, **Role / title** and **Stage**, then select **Save job**. Open **More details** for optional fields. Application and follow-up dates use the native date picker; **Clear date** removes an optional follow-up date.
- **Keep unfinished forms.** Dismissing an edited job or interview form asks whether to **Discard** or **Keep editing**. Save and Cancel remain at the bottom. While a save runs, wait for its result before leaving.
- **Choose the next task.** Application details offer a task such as **Prepare application**, **Prepare for interview**, or **Draft a follow-up**. Stage progress is informational; select **Change stage** to edit it.
- **Switch Knowledge views.** **Facts**, **Stories** and **Preferences** appear together. Workspace tabs reveal the selected destination and provide arrows when the full strip does not fit.
- **Refresh saved information.** Pull down in Documents, Applications (including Board, Calendar and Insights), Career or account settings. In Board, pull inside a stage column. Check also supports pulling down to rerun its local checks.
- **Choose any timezone.** Open **Settings → Preferences → Timezone**, then search by region or city. **Show more** reveals another batch of timezones. Interview forms offer the same searchable list and can keep a separate timezone for an interview.
- **Return from document options.** Rename, duplicate, notes and details offer **Back** to return to the document menu. Swipe the sheet down to dismiss it.

Larger text reflows collection controls and settings values. Scroll the Documents list to reach filters, errors and documents rather than shrinking your reading size.
