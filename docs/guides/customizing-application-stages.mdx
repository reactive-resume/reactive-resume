title: "Customizing application stages"
description: "Add, rename, recolor, reorder and remove the stages on your application board, including a Ghosted stage for applications that never got a reply."
---

Your applications start with six standard stages: **Saved**, **Applied**, **Screening**, **Interview**, **Offer** and **Closed**. You can rename and recolor them, change their order, and add your own, such as **Take-home assignment** or **Ghosted**. Your stages appear in the List, Board, the details sheet, the application workspace and the mobile app.

## How custom stages count

Every stage you add **counts as** one of the six standard stages. That choice decides how the application behaves:

| Counts as     | What it means                                                                                                                              |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Saved**     | Not applied yet. No documents are recorded as sent.                                                                                        |
| **Applied**   | Sent. The documents you confirm are saved as sent, and the "No reply in _n_ days" reminder can appear.                                     |
| **Screening** | Sent and heard back.                                                                                                                       |
| **Interview** | Sent and interviewing.                                                                                                                     |
| **Offer**     | An offer is on the table.                                                                                                                  |
| **Closed**    | Finished. It's hidden until you turn on **Show closed applications**, and it has no next step. A Closed stage can also preset its outcome. |

For example, **Take-home assignment** could count as **Interview**, and **Ghosted** counts as **Closed** with the outcome **No response**. Insights uses what each stage counts as, never its name or position, so renaming or reordering stages doesn't change your numbers.

What a stage counts as can't be changed after you add it. To change it, add a new stage and remove the old one.

## Open your stages

On the **Applications** page, select the **Manage stages** button (the sliders icon) next to **Save job**. The **Stages** sheet lists your stages in board order.

## Rename or recolor a stage

- To rename, edit the name in the list and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or select elsewhere. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to cancel. Names can be up to 80 characters.
- To recolor, select the color square at the start of the row and pick a color.

You can rename and recolor the standard stages too. They keep their meaning.

## Change the order

Use the **Move up** and **Move down** buttons on each row. The order sets the columns on the Board, the steps in the details sheet and what **Move to _next stage_** offers. Closed stages are skipped by **Move to _next stage_**; mark an application closed with **Mark as closed…** instead.

## Add a stage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name it">Under **Add a stage**, type a **Name** and pick a color.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose what it counts as">
    Choose one of the six standard stages under **Counts as**. For a Closed stage, choose an **Outcome**, or **Ask each
    time** to pick the reason whenever an application is closed there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Add stage">The new stage appears at the end of the list and on the Board.</Step>
</Steps>

To add a **Ghosted** stage in one step, select **Add Ghosted (closed, no response)**. Applications moved there close with the outcome **No response**.

You can have up to 40 stages.

Combining a guest account with an existing account must also fit this limit. If it would exceed 40 stages, sign-in stops and keeps your guest session and data available. Remove unused custom stages before trying again.

## Remove a stage

Select **Remove** (the trash icon) on a stage you added, choose where its applications go, and select **Remove stage**. Only stages that count the same, with the same preset outcome, can take them. If there isn't one, add it first. Standard stages can't be removed.

Removing a stage doesn't delete applications or their history; earlier activity still shows the name the stage had.

## Use your stages

- **Board**: each stage is a column. Drag a card between columns, including between two stages that count the same. Each card's **⋯** menu has **Move to…** and **Mark as closed…** for the keyboard.
- **List**: applications are grouped by stage. Select rows to move or close them together.
- **Details sheet**: select a stage in the stepper, or **Move to _next stage_**. With many stages, the stepper scrolls sideways. **Mark as closed…** lets you pick a Closed stage you added.
- **Adding an application**: choose any of your Saved, Applied or Interview stages.
- **Edit details…**: the **Stage** field lists all your stages.

Moving between two stages that count the same, such as **Interview** and **Take-home assignment**, is added to the activity history. It doesn't reset the "No reply" reminder or change your applied date.

Closing an application pauses its Career schedules and pending notifications. Reopening resumes them; an overdue schedule can run on the next scheduler tick. Review or pause schedules before reopening if you don't want them to resume. An email already being sent cannot be recalled.

## A warning when you've been ghosted before

When you save a job or mark it as applied, Reactive Resume checks your earlier applications to the same company that ended with **No response**. If it finds any, it lists them so you can decide whether to apply again. It's only advice: nothing is blocked. Company names are matched as written, ignoring capitals and extra spaces, so a differently named branch of the same company isn't recognized.

Recorded No response outcomes remain available for this warning after you reopen an application. Outcomes cleared before outcome history was introduced cannot be recovered.

## Older servers

If your self-hosted server doesn't support custom stages yet, the Applications page uses the six standard stages and the **Manage stages** button is hidden. Update the server to use custom stages.
