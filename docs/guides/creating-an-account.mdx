You need a free account to save resumes, cover letters and job applications. Signing up takes under a minute, and you can start building right after.

## Sign up with email and password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-up page">
    Go to [rxresu.me](https://rxresu.me) and select **Build your resume**. The **Start your resume** sign-up page opens. If you are on the sign-in page instead, select **Create one now**.

    If you run your own copy of Reactive Resume, use its address instead of `rxresu.me`.

  </Step>

<Step title="Fill in your details">
  - **Name**: your name as you want it shown in the app (up to 64 characters). - **Username** (optional): 3 to 64
  characters, using Latin letters a–z, numbers, dots, hyphens and underscores. It becomes part of every public resume
  link, for example `rxresu.me/alexmorgan/game-developer`. Leave it empty and one is made from your email address (with
  a number added if it's taken). - **Email address**: an address you can open. You need it to reset a forgotten
  password. - **Password**: at least 8 characters, up to 64. The rule is shown under the field.
</Step>

<Step title="Select Sign up">
  Your account is created, you are signed in, and **Documents** opens straight away (or the page that sent you to sign
  up). A **Check your email** message says a verification link is on its way. Verifying is optional: open the link from
  your inbox whenever you like.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can change your name, username and email later in **Settings → Account**. Changing your username changes every
  public link you have shared; old links send visitors to the new address for 30 days, then stop. Pick one you are happy
  to keep.
</Tip>

## Try it without an account

Where the site allows guests, **Try it without an account** under **Build your resume** on the homepage (and
**Continue as guest** on the sign-in page) opens Documents in a temporary guest session. Create an account later to
keep what you made.

## Keep your work from a guest session

If you tried Reactive Resume as a guest, select **Create account** or **Sign in** on the guest banner. The sign-up and
sign-in pages then say what happens: your guest resumes, cover letters and applications (with their history, your
private application profile and saved answers) and your Career knowledge (facts, stories and profile) move to the
account. A fact the account already has stays as it is there, and if the account already has a Career profile, it
keeps its own; those guest copies are deleted. Assistant conversations don't move either; they're deleted with the
guest session. Once you're in, a message confirms the move.

## Sign up with a social account

Under **or continue with**, select **Google**, **GitHub** or **LinkedIn** and approve the request on that site. Reactive Resume creates your account from the name, email and photo the provider shares, and picks a username for you. You can change it afterwards in **Settings → Account**.

These buttons only appear when the site has set them up. On a self-hosted copy you may see none of them, or a single button for your organization's sign-in service.

## If sign-up doesn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The username or email is already taken">
    Usernames and email addresses are unique. Try another username. If the email is yours, you already have an account: go back to the sign-in page and use **Forgot password?** to get back in.
  </Accordion>

<Accordion title="The verification email didn't arrive">
  Check your spam folder. You can ask for a new link from **Settings → Account**: under the **Email** field, select
  **Not verified yet. Resend the link**. On self-hosted copies without email delivery, the field says so instead, and no
  email is sent.
</Accordion>

  <Accordion title="There is no Create one now link">
    The site's owner has turned off new sign-ups. Only existing accounts can sign in.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
