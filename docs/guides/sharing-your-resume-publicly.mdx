---
title: "Sharing your resume as a web page"
description: "Publish your resume as a web page, choose its address, decide who can find it and what they see, and see how many people visited."
---

The **Share** mode publishes your resume as a web page that anyone you send the link to can open in a browser, without an account. The page uses your resume's template and Design, fits any screen, and always shows your latest changes. Resumes are private until you publish them.

## Before you start

- Open the resume in the editor. Web pages belong to resumes; cover letters don't have one.
- Check your username in **Settings** → **Account**. It's part of every public address, for example `https://rxresu.me/dkowalski/game-developer`. If you host Reactive Resume yourself, your own address replaces `rxresu.me`.

## Publish your web page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Share">
    In the editor bar, select **Share**, next to Write, Design and Check (or press <kbd>4</kbd>, or <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> on Windows and Linux). On a phone, select **Share** in the tab bar at the bottom.

    The page area shows your web page as visitors will see it. Switch between **Computer** and **Phone** to check both.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Publish">
    Select **Publish**. The page is live at once, and **Share** in the editor bar gains a dot so you can tell at a glance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the address">
    Select **Copy** next to the **Address** field and paste the link into an email, a job application or your LinkedIn profile. On a phone or tablet you can also select **Share via…**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Open web page** opens it in a new tab, and **QR code** shows a code that opens the link, handy for a printed card or a slide. In the mobile app, **Preview web page** opens the page in the app's browser, even before you publish it.

## What visitors see

- **On a computer**, the resume sits on the page like a sheet of paper, in your template.
- **On a phone**, the same design narrows to the screen: your header first, then the main column, then the sidebar.
- **Download PDF** and **Share** stay at the bottom of the screen. Download PDF only shows when visitors can download it.
- **With a password**, visitors first see **This resume is password-protected**, enter the password you shared and select **Unlock**. The page stays unlocked for about 10 minutes.
- **When the page isn't published**, or the resume is in Trash, visitors see **This resume isn't shared right now.** The page gives nothing away about you.

When someone pastes your link into LinkedIn, Slack or a chat, the preview shows your name, headline and photo in your resume's colours.

Visitors never see your private notes, or any section or entry you've hidden. A small **Made with Reactive Resume, free and open source** line sits under the page; you can turn it off under **Web page**.

## Change the address

The last part of the address is yours to choose. It starts out based on the resume's name.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit the Address field">
    With the page published, type a new ending in the **Address** field. Use lowercase letters, numbers and single
    dashes; spaces turn into dashes as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the check">
    Reactive Resume checks the address as you type and saves it once it's free. Until then, the old address keeps
    working.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If another of your resumes already uses that address, the message names it and suggests a free one. After a change, the old address still leads to your resume for 30 days. Changing your username is different: every public address and your short link change at once, and old links stop working.

## Decide who can find it and what they see

Everything under **Privacy** applies to the web page, its PDF, link previews and search results.

| Setting                           | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Require a password**            | Visitors enter a password you share separately before they see anything.                                                                                                                                    |
| **Hide contact details**          | Leaves out your email, phone, location and other header details, and references' phone numbers.                                                                                                             |
| **Let visitors ask to see them**  | With contact details hidden, a **Show contact details** button reveals them to a visitor who selects it. Search engines never see them.                                                                     |
| **Show in search engines**        | Off by default. Turn it on to let search engines such as Google list your page. It can take weeks to appear, and weeks to disappear after you turn it off. Not available while the resume needs a password. |
| **Visitors can download the PDF** | On by default. Turn it off and the page has no Download PDF button.                                                                                                                                         |

<Tip>
  Turning on **Show in search engines** with **Hide contact details** lets recruiters find you without your phone number
  or address ending up in search results.
</Tip>

## Short link and your own domain

On rxresu.me, one resume can be your **Primary resume**. It opens at your short link, `rx.cv/<username>`, and at your own domain if you connect one.

- Turn on **Primary resume** in Share. The resume must be published first. If another resume was primary, Reactive Resume asks before moving the role.
- **Copy** next to your short link to paste it anywhere. It always leads to your current primary resume.
- To use your own domain, type it under **Your domain** and select **Connect**. Add the two DNS records shown where you manage the domain, then select **Check DNS**. A subdomain such as `resume.example.com` uses a CNAME record; a root domain such as `example.com` uses A and AAAA records instead. Changes can take a few hours to reach everyone.

Once connected, your domain shows your primary resume, and search engines are pointed to your domain rather than the rxresu.me address. If your domain's records stop pointing at Reactive Resume for a week, it's disconnected.

## See who visited

The bottom of Share shows **Visitors**. Only you see these numbers.

| Number                        | What it counts                                                               |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **views · 30 days**           | Visits to your web page in the last 30 days                                  |
| **downloads**                 | PDFs visitors downloaded from the page in the last 30 days                   |
| **asked for contact details** | Visitors who selected **Show contact details**, when your details are hidden |
| **since last view**           | How long ago someone last opened the page, such as `5m`, `2h` or `3d`        |

- Your own visits and downloads don't count while you're signed in, and search engines and other bots never count.
- Repeat visits from the same visitor within an hour count once.
- Counts are anonymous. Reactive Resume doesn't record who viewed your resume.

## Stop sharing

Turn off **Public link** in Share. The page stops working immediately, and visitors see **This resume isn't shared right now.** Your address is kept, so publishing again brings the same address back. Moving the resume to Trash also stops its page.

<Note>
  While a resume is locked, you can't change its Share settings. Select the resume's name in the editor bar, then
  **Unlock editing** first. Share needs a connection.
</Note>
