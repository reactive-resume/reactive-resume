title: "Entering dates"
description: "Type start and end dates, year-only dates and current roles in Reactive Resume, save custom date text, and choose how dates print."
---

Use month-and-year date fields for consistent formatting, sorting and date checks, or turn on **Custom date text** to write dates in your own words. Both options are available on the web and in the native mobile app.

## Enter dates for an entry

Experience, Education, Projects and Volunteer entries have a start and an end date. Each job role has its own pair too.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the entry">
    In **Write** mode, open the entry you want to date. See [Editing entries](/guides/editing-entries) if you're not
    sure how.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the start date">
    In the first box under **Dates**, type the date the way you normally write it, for example `Mar 2022`, `March 2022`,
    `03/2022`, `2022-03`, or only `2022`. A date that can be read saves as you type. When you leave the box, it switches
    to your resume's date format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the end date, or turn on Present">
    Type the end date in the second box. If you're still in the role or still studying, turn on **Present** instead. The
    end box then shows the word "Present" and can't be edited. On an Education entry whose end date is still to come (a
    degree you're finishing), type that date and turn on **Expected**: it prints as "Expected May 2027".
  </Step>
</Steps>

Awards, Certifications and Publications have a single **Date** box and no Present switch.

## Custom date text

Turn on **Custom date text** in an entry's **Dates** or **Date** field. The current date becomes one editable text box. For a range, type the whole range, including any separator or word for "Present". You can use localized wording, a season, or any other text.

Custom text saves as you type and prints as written in the preview, shared resume, PDF and DOCX. Changing the resume's language or date format does not reformat it.

If the text cannot be read, a **Saved as written** warning explains that automated date checks and sorting are unavailable for it. You can keep the text and ignore the finding in **Check** mode. The warning does not block saving or exporting.

To return to the date fields, enter a readable month and year, year-only date or range, or clear the custom text, then turn off **Custom date text**. Untouched preset text can always return to the date fields. For edited custom text, the switch stays disabled while it cannot be converted exactly, so returning to date fields cannot silently discard it.

## Year-only dates

If you don't remember the month, or prefer not to show it, type only the year, such as `2014`. Year-only dates are exact, not guesses: they print as the year alone whatever date format you choose, for example "2014 – 2018".

## How dates print

The page shows the start and end joined by a dash. If there's only a start date, only that date prints; with **Present** on, the end reads "Present", and with **Expected** on (Education only), the end date reads "Expected May 2027".

Month names and the word "Present" follow the resume's language, which you set in **Design** → **Page** → **Language**. For example, a resume in German prints "März 2022 – Heute". You can also type month names in the resume's language.

## Choose the date format

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Advanced in Design mode">
    Select **Design** in the editor bar, then open **Advanced** at the bottom of the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a date format">
    Choose one of the four formats in **Date format**. Every date using the date fields changes at once. Custom date
    text stays as written.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Choose how structured dates print">
  <img
    src="/images/guides/entering-dates/design-advanced-date-format.webp"
    alt="Advanced Design area with a Date format selector and typography controls below"
  />
</Frame>

| Option         | Prints as                               |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Mar 2022**   | Short month name and year (the default) |
| **March 2022** | Full month name and year                |
| **03/2022**    | Month number and year                   |
| **2022-03**    | Year and month number (ISO)             |

Resumes brought over from an earlier version of Reactive Resume start with whichever of these formats is closest to how their dates were typed. **Reset design defaults** in the same panel doesn't change your date format.

## Fix dates that need a look

The Dates field tells you when something is off.

- **"This date has not been saved."** The text you typed cannot be read. Correct it using a month and year, such as Mar 2022, or only a year. The page keeps the last readable date until then. Turn on **Custom date text** to save other wording.
- **"The end is before the start."** The two dates are the wrong way round. Swap them, or the entry sorts to the end when you use **Sort by date**.

### Dates from imports and older resumes

When you import a resume, or open one made before dates were structured, Reactive Resume converts each date from its text. Dates it cannot read exactly, such as "Summer 2016", stay as custom text:

- The section's row shows a **to check** badge only for nonempty date text without a parsed start. Readable custom text and empty dates do not add to the badge.
- The entry opens with **Custom date text** turned on, showing the original wording for editing.
- The entry keeps printing that text as written. Unreadable dates show a non-blocking warning.

Keep the custom text, edit it, or enter a readable date and turn off **Custom date text** to use the date fields.

<Note>
  **Check** mode also flags entries without dates, dates that can't be read, dates that run backwards and dates in the
  future. See [Checking your resume](/guides/checking-your-resume).
</Note>
