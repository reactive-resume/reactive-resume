---
title: "Find roles while you are busy"
description: "Set a discovery schedule, review open postings and track only the jobs you choose."
---

You have a job already and little time to scroll job boards. Set up a search once, then review new roles in **Career → Today** when you have time.

Discovery reads new search results to check that they are individual open job postings. It skips results it cannot confirm as postings, including recognized closed roles. A posting can close after that check, so read it before applying. The number of new roles varies; there is no promised daily quota.

## Set what you are looking for

In **Career → Knowledge → Preferences**, record target roles, places, minimum base salary, maximum office days and what matters to you. These help explain matches and unknown details.

**Where you're looking for work** sets the countries web searches target, up to three. It starts with the country of your account timezone, marked **From your timezone**. If your account is still on UTC and you have never set search countries, your first search or enabled discovery schedule saves the country of your device timezone. Select **Add a country** to search for more, or remove every country to search anywhere. **Use my timezone** returns to the automatic country.

Enter salary as a full amount, such as **95000**, rather than **95k**. Invalid shorthand stays visible until corrected. Choose **Any** for maximum office days when you have no requirement; it clears a previous choice.

Set your timezone in **Settings → Preferences**. Schedule times use that timezone.

Discovery needs a web search connection or server-configured SearXNG search. Check **Settings → AI & developer → Web access**, or ask your installation's administrator if services are configured centrally. AI text coaching is a separate connection. It is not required just to schedule a discovery search.

## Schedule the search

1. Open **Career → Today**.
2. In **Schedules**, select **Add**, then **Find roles**.
3. Edit **Look for**. It starts from your preferences; include roles, places or companies to focus the search.
4. Choose **Every day**, **Every 3 days** or **Every week**.
5. Set **First run** and **Time**. For morning results, choose a morning time such as 08:00.
6. Leave **Turn this schedule on** enabled and select **Save schedule**.

The server runs the schedule while your browser is closed. Search services may charge for usage. See [Schedule controls](/guides/career-workspace-reference#schedules) for delivery and email options.

## Review the roles on Today

When discovery finishes, **Needs you** tells you there are results to review. Under **New roles from your search**, each role has a summary, posting link and match details.

Read match chips as a starting point. **Salary not listed** or **Office days not stated** means the posting did not establish that detail. It is a question to resolve, not confirmation that your preference is met.

For each role:

- **Read posting** to inspect the job before deciding.
- **Track in Applications** to create a Saved application with its posting and match notes. This does not submit an application.
- **Not for me** to dismiss it. Use **Undo** if you change your mind.

Once tracked, [check Fit](/guides/checking-job-fit) using your experience, then prepare the application.

## Adjust or pause the search

Select a schedule's row to edit its search, frequency or time. Use its switch to pause it without losing those settings. Delete it when you no longer need it; the notice offers **Undo**.

Pausing or changing a schedule cancels its old work still in progress. A cancelled run does not publish a briefing or notification.

Untracked postings found again are rechecked after a week. Changed descriptions or recognized closures update the existing role without another new-role notice.

Changing your preferences updates how role details are matched. Review the schedule's **Look for** text separately when your search direction changes; it was copied from preferences when you created the schedule.

If nothing new appears, check that the schedule is on and when it last ran. A completed search can have no new usable postings. For a failure notice, check the web connection and [troubleshooting](/guides/career-workspace-reference#when-something-does-not-work).
