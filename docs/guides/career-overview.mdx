You have an interview this afternoon, another application form to finish, and a recruiter waiting for a reply. Career helps you carry the same experience through all three, without starting over each time.

Start with facts you know are true and stories about work you have done. Use them across your search, then keep each job's posting, answers, interview preparation and messages in its own workspace.

## Two places, two purposes

**Career** in the sidebar is for your whole search:

- **Today** brings together reminders, new roles and your next interview, with the two after it listed underneath. Notifications you have read are removed after 90 days; unread ones stay until you read them. When your account timezone isn't your device's, times show their zone ("16:56 UTC") and Today offers **Use this device's timezone**.
- **Coach** is a conversation about your search across jobs.
- **Knowledge** holds your facts, stories and preferences.
- **Offers** compares saved offers side by side.

**Applications** is your job tracker. Open an application and select **Open workspace** for tools dedicated to that job: **Fit**, **Apply**, **Interview prep**, **Practice**, **Debrief**, **Messages** and **Library**. **Ask the coach** opens a conversation beside the tab you are using, with that application's context.

Coach starts with **What should we work on?** and suggested questions. Its connection menu shows the connection and
model. Choose context in the message box and read the disclosure underneath: it names what will be sent and what the
coach may remember. The document [assistant](/guides/using-the-assistant) uses the same controls with its own document context.

When the coach asks a follow-up question, select one of its choices or send your own answer. It continues with that answer on web and mobile. See [Answer a question](/guides/using-the-assistant#answer-a-question) for reconnecting, retrying and unfinished questions.

Scroll up to read earlier messages without following new text. **Latest** returns you to the newest message and resumes following. It disappears when you reach the bottom, including a small gap left as scrolling settles, and stays out of the keyboard tab order while hidden.

Choose a saved conversation's title to rename it in **Conversations**. On web, Enter saves and Escape cancels; in the native app, use **Save** or **Cancel**.

When an interview is upcoming, **Practice in [company]'s workspace** opens that round's questions in a three-question Practice session. The link appears in new and saved conversations, works without an AI connection, and preserves your unsent draft. It uses the workspace's saved or general questions rather than copying chat text.

**Needs you** lists unread notices, such as finished briefings and new roles, together with applications that need you now. These come from the same rules as each application's **Next step** in [Applications](/guides/tracking-job-applications): a follow-up due within a day or overdue, no reply 10 days after applying, an interview in the next week, or an offer to decide. Select **Open workspace** to act on one. The list is recalculated from each application's resulting **Next step**. A due follow-up can keep an application listed. Clearing one may reveal another qualifying step, such as no reply after 10 days or an offer to decide.

**Coming up** shows the next three dated steps across open applications: interviews, application follow-ups and reply deadlines from each application's latest saved offer. Dates use your account timezone. Several steps from one application can appear together. Interviews stay until they finish; overdue follow-ups and offer deadlines belong in **Needs you**. **Open workspace** opens the interview round in **Interview prep**, or the application in **Messages** for a follow-up or offer reply. Web and native mobile use the same ordering.

For the next future interview within 24 hours, Coming up also offers **Generate briefing now** and **Practice 3 questions**, even when an earlier reminder leads the card. Read the company, time and disclosure first. Generation saves a briefing through your default AI connection without creating a preparation schedule; Practice opens without an AI request. [Prepare now from Today](/guides/preparing-for-interview-day#prepare-now-from-today).

## Keep your experience honest

A **fact** is something you state or confirm, such as what you did, when you did it, or a result you measured. A **story** puts that experience into five parts: Situation, Task, Action, Result and Reflection. Link stories to the facts that support them.

These are your records, not independent checks by an employer or a background-check service. You decide whether each fact is accurate. Leave a story's Result blank when you cannot support it. If you correct a linked fact later, the story is flagged for review.

**Fit** shows the evidence behind each requirement. **No evidence yet** means your Knowledge does not show it; it does not mean you cannot do the job. A related experience can be useful without becoming a claim that you have done something you have not.

AI drafts are built from the experience you supply. Read them before using them, especially names, dates, your personal contribution and results. [Build your Knowledge](/guides/building-career-knowledge), or [check a role when your experience is different](/guides/checking-job-fit).

## Tell it once, use it again

The same shared fact or story can help with an application answer, an interview opening, a practice answer or a recruiter reply. You still review each draft for its audience.

Job-specific notes stay with that application. A fact marked private to one application stays in that scope until you choose **Use for all applications**. Shared Knowledge lets you reuse experience without treating every conversation with an employer as a fact about every job.

## Let scheduled work help

Enable schedules when you want discovery searches, interview briefings or follow-up reminders to run while your browser is closed. Results wait in **Today** for you to review. Finding a role does not apply for it; reading a message does not send a reply.

Optional role suggestions in Knowledge → Preferences stay editable drafts until you use and save them. Discovery cards distinguish phrase and keyword overlap from optional AI Fit, and expose source and check times without promising freshness. Bulk track or dismiss keeps individual failures visible for review.

Schedules need a working installation and the relevant connections. Discovery checks that new results are individual open postings when it reads them. A posting can close afterward, and a search may find few or no new roles. [Set up a quiet search](/guides/finding-roles-automatically) or [prepare for interview day](/guides/preparing-for-interview-day).

## Choose your AI and control your data

Reactive Resume is free and open source. You can use your own AI connection, including OpenAI, DeepSeek, Google Gemini or another supported provider, and choose a default. Provider usage can cost money; it is billed through the connection you use. [Connect an AI provider](/guides/using-ai).

Each AI action has a line explaining what it sends and which connection receives it. In the coach, context controls let you choose what accompanies the next message. Knowledge editing and the offer comparison itself do not send an AI request. Previously sent information remains subject to your provider's policies.

You can correct facts, switch them off for coaching, forget them, and export your account data. You can also [self-host Reactive Resume](/self-hosting/docker). See [controls and data reference](/guides/career-workspace-reference#your-data) for the different effects of these actions.

### Documents and application details

Use **Stage, details and timeline** in the workspace to open the application record, change its stage,
edit details, or log a dated update. **Notes** holds things to remember and share with the coach;
**Log an update** adds a dated timeline entry.

For a saved job, **Use an existing letter** links a letter from Documents. Documents also offers
**Link to application…** for resumes and letters, and **Write a letter…** on a resume. When starting
a new letter, choose **Resume for a new letter**, or **No resume** for a standalone letter.
A letter recorded as sent keeps its application link; pickers label unavailable targets **Letter already sent**.
Builders offer a back link to their application. Submitted versions remain immutable; current drafts stay editable.
